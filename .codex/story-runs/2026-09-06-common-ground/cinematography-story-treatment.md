## What the new movement must change

The strongest inherited image is an auditor whose body moves like the feed. The strongest unused opportunity is to let his straight line encounter a place the player already knows as alive. The new image should be **a route that becomes usable because one person changes the evidence and the other makes a correction of his own**. The trees, water, sky and other mind remain themselves.

At present, installation ends by consuming the cell and clearing the objective (`CG-C01`; `stationReturnStory.ts:109–137`). First give that journey a local, credible visual completion: the established core acknowledges the actual committed install through a small material/mechanical state change, and its light remains steady while the player can turn away. Do not make home flicker again to purchase another departure. A restored save begins with that state already true and never replays the cell transaction or its completion flourish.

The new Origin visit should pay off the tree, pond and absent auditor identified in the blind report's loose threads. It must not validate that reader's unsupported theories that the cell is a surveillance leash, that the station is W-7744's employer, or that organic-sample surrender killed animals. The original audit source commits inventory samples; those cold-reader interpretations are perception evidence, not permission to manufacture consequences.

Proposed dramatic reading, for Chapter's decision: Terra wants to show that the living site can be inhabited without erasing it. W-7744 wants a repeatable inspection that lets him retain his model. Terra first assumes that showing the destination and going there should suffice; he treats an unrepeatable demonstration as inadequate evidence. Terra learns to make the intermediate steps legible. W-7744 does not accept Terra's whole account; he makes one bounded procedural concession by correcting a route and testing it himself. His motive for returning is his own unresolved prior audit, never knowledge obtained from the station/cell. This precise motive and the extent of concession require the signed delegated canon decision.

## Selected visual dramaturgy

### 1. A home that can be left without being abandoned

The completion at Tidegarden is close and ordinary. The core, its existing brass/teal form and the shelter opening should coexist in a player-owned view when the player's position permits it. The cue belongs to the core's own prewarmed material/mechanical detail; no new light, whole-screen glow, exposure wash, FOV squeeze or forced turn. One visible latch/status change should answer the actual install. Keep the warm state afterwards so turning back can confirm that home remains reliable.

If the player installs while looking into a wall, do not seize the camera or fake completion elsewhere. The interaction must already have required a credible view/reach, and the short acknowledgment remains available as ordinary object state when they turn back. Chapter/Integration own the interaction and completion receipt; Score owns the audible answer. Cinema requires the persistent object-level state and a clean guidance handoff. Exact treatment of existing restored saves must give recognition of the finished home without pretending an installation just happened.

The move back to Origin has a Chapter-authored reason available through the existing course/relay vocabulary. It must not be a fresh emergency or a summons threatening the restored hearth. Cinema supplies no ominous station cutaway, watching lens, tracker glow or new administrative counter to make that trip feel necessary. The player boards and flies through the existing vehicle camera; there is no second travel movie or new sky/reality reveal.

### 2. The old tree, and a man who is still looking past it

Re-entry finds W-7744 conducting a repeat inspection at the old audit landscape. Bring him into view through walking, turning and a reachable landmark; do not respawn him at the old relay merely because an A4 receipt exists. His renewed presence has its own ch13 lifetime and one pose writer.

Use the old tree/pond/relay triangle as spatial memory. W-7744 is first read when he becomes relevant, the tree is second read, and the route between them remains open enough to understand on foot. His ember slit is the scarce accent. Keep his gait rigid, suit unchanged and voice unsoftened. His first meaningful difference from the earlier audit is an action: he repeats a physical measurement, changes his position, then repeats it. Avoid an idle animation that merely signifies generic confusion.

A small manufactured sighting head/standard is acceptable chapter-local apparatus if Chapter adopts it. Its visible orientation can pass the tree and settle on a far solid marker while Terra's ordinary living view contains the trunk/foliage in the interval. This exposes attention and classification, not a correct hidden world. No instrument screen paints everything he sees; no X-ray ray penetrates all geometry; no camera jumps into his eyes. The precise sighting mechanic must be bounded and based on known objects, and its interpretation remains incomplete. His past bare blink is not replayed as a reusable power.

### 3. Put a route where another body can test it

Candidate playable apparatus: **three reusable manufactured survey markers**, distinct by physical notch/fin shape and readable without hue. W-7744 has brought the apparatus for his inspection; it is available where he is working, with no crafting fee or collection quota. Chapter decides its fiction and exact verbs. Cinematography requires visible physical placement and recoverability, not menu-only coordinate submission.

The player can carry, place, relocate and demonstrate a route using the same F interaction family. Placed markers have a simple stable orientation and a short local sign of the next leg, not a glowing ribbon that gives the answer. The active marker retains the existing HUD designator; other pieces are physical objects. No new controls map, secondary targeting screen, duplicated compass or awareness meter.

The proposed route must remain a proposal until W-7744 has physically tested it. A dry-looking short cut can be traversable by Terra's jump while failing his grounded stride, or vegetation can hide a terrain discontinuity that a superficial demonstration misses. The visual disagreement is specific: Terra attends to the living route; his instrument attends to repeatable manufactured reference and ground. **Do not make his ignorance mechanically equivalent to random refusal.** He must stop at the last supported point and orient to the exact missing/invalid leg. The cause can be inspected in Terra's normal view.

Mandatory physical tests use shared terrain support, slope, clearance and marker relations. Foliage silhouette may supply the perceptual context but cannot be the only collision or success rule unless its physical authority is contracted and tested. No invisible “he cannot see life” wall is acceptable. Do not change fauna or water systems merely to create a puzzle obstacle.

Revision reuses the same pieces and preserved valid legs. A player can walk back to the refusal point, compare the previously placed marker with the auditor's sighting direction, and move one marker onto a better dry route. The cost of a mistaken proposal is a halted crossing and the need to understand why; not lost resources, a destroyed home, arbitrary health damage or resetting the whole route. More than one terrain-valid placement must be accepted within the bounded work area. The HUD tells the available action and reason for refusal, never the successful coordinate.

### 4. A correction Terra did not order

After the player has made and tested the useful part of the route, W-7744 must do more than walk it. Proposed payoff: he relocates the final unoccupied marker a short, physically valid distance to a different supported approach, sights the changed leg, and takes it. Terra has neither issued that coordinate nor triggered a “make friend” action. He is improving a procedure he can defend; the living route remains larger than his explanation.

This is a authored independent act with deterministic bounded alternatives, not free-form AI. The candidate must give him a real geometrically different move, selected from his own ground/clearance evidence, that still respects the player's accepted work. His independent leg may not auto-solve an untested player route. It must occur after actual traversal receipts and remain valid under live terrain, reload and the chosen marker arrangement. If no candidate is valid, he holds with an actionable reason; time cannot grant it.

The emotional image is small: a rigid body moves one of the player's signs, then trusts that changed ground enough to put his weight on it. No smile, visor turning friendly blue, matched soul colors, aura, mirror body or new first-person camera signals an awakening. Do not make his final stance face Terra as if awaiting approval. He faces the route he has chosen.

### 5. Aftermath that remains in the place

Keep the placed/changed route as a modest physical consequence on Origin. Markers are reusable objects with a bounded persistent snapshot; they do not become a permanent luminous quest trail. The scene should allow the player to retrace both versions, look at the still-unresolved tree, or leave. Chapter owns the exact final line and the future task, if any. Cinema requests an interval without a new objective immediately covering the changed path.

W-7744 remains opposed and partly opaque. His exit/continued presence is explicitly contracted: a safe visible departure toward his work area, then retirement only when genuinely offstage/occluded or on world exit; do not pop him out while being watched. If the player follows, keep the single body in a bounded idle/hold state until the normal world boundary. Do not revive A4 flight/pack choreography. Completed-state geometry may persist; transient camera, cue and new-objective authority must be inert after completion.

No cinematic time-of-day jump is necessary; authored routes must be readable by daylight and under the existing night light budget, or the chapter must expose an honest availability window while allowing free play. None acquires a “friendship” palette at completion. The familiar place can feel newly authored because the characters act differently in it.

## Guidance, agency and failure contract

The blind reader asks for a quieter HUD. Preserve that intent without dropping the executable guidance contract. Keep one standing work order for the current verb and one exact matching marker. Guidance is procedural and attributable to the player's current task; W-7744's claims stay on the existing audit band. Do not label player-authored placement with the same presentation as an auditor's command, or write that no order exists while publishing an imperative to a prescribed answer.

Candidate action categories for Chapter to name: take/place/move a marker; demonstrate or offer a route; inspect a held leg; revise; physically retest. Marker destination is the apparatus, the player's selected piece, or the actual refusal locus. It is not a secret optimal solution. A held state must state a concrete reason such as unsupported step, broken leg or obscured manufactured reference, then the available correction. Never merely “WAIT UNTIL HE UNDERSTANDS.” Exact IDs, labels and all copy remain Chapter-authored contract work.

Free camera and movement are maintained through every action. A player who walks away leaves a real held route, not a countdown that finishes offscreen. A player who looks away can return to persistent evidence; the scene does not repeat the same emotional action indefinitely to catch the camera. Pause/focus freezes active actor presentation/traversal consistently. Carry/place and offer actions use the existing rising F edge and arbitration; build mode must release or explicitly own its conflicting F action. A contextual action cannot silently steal a block-placement command.

Failure is reversible and locally meaningful. Reject unsafe placement without consuming an item. An obstructed actor holds before invalid ground, not inside it. On revision, only uncommitted legs are revalidated/replaced; accepted progress persists unless actual terrain edits invalidate its support. Preserve and expose that invalidation. No refusal branch may kill fauna, drain the hearth, demote reality, or force a second collection chain. A successful first proposal need not be deliberately sabotaged: the independent correction can still reveal W-7744's distinct reading; failure is available and must be tested, not a compulsory humiliation.

Manual and movie use identical placement, offer and actor-traversal predicates. Movie may demonstrate one valid solution and a separate diagnostic refusal/retry path; it may not inject success, move the body over invalid terrain or count a timeout as agreement. Continuous scope includes authentic station departure, return, landing and installation, since the current movie ends in the station (`common-packet.md`, `integration-survey.md`). The typed external ch11/ch12 transition remains distinct from formal ch13 entry.

## Device, motion and tier equivalence

- **Lowest tier:** retain the same three manufactured pieces, actor, physical support and outcome. Neither postprocessing nor fauna/grass density decides the route. Test the mechanic in a frame where no high-tier foliage or grade survives; it must still explain the held and revised leg. Ecology enriches the contrast without withholding the usable evidence.

Optional marker shimmer is disabled; stable fins/poses and exact labels carry state. No accessibility mode removes a relevant target or changes success predicates.
- **Mobile:** use the existing touch glyph substitution and action button. Carry/place preview operates within the same short reach; it cannot demand pixel-accurate aiming at a tiny peg. Keep target touch/interaction volumes generous while geometric validation stays exact. Show actor/prop contact above controls and outside caption/work-order rectangles, using measured safe areas rather than fixed borrowed margins.
- **Non-auditory access:** a held foot/body, instrument/marker geometry and specific work-order reason communicate failure; a sound cannot be its only signal. Completion leaves tested geometry even if the score is muted. Color-deficient viewing retains material value and shape contrast.

# Continuation acceptance requirements

These are verification obligations for the commissioned continuation, not new canon or a signed scene treatment.

1. An existing save with the second hearth restored reaches the next authored action without replaying cell issuance, consuming another cell, losing the home, or restarting an earlier chapter. Saves before that point retain their actual progress.
2. The station visit and return have explicit, truthful transition ownership. The chapter registry must not claim that arrival at the station already guarantees restoration at Tidegarden. Preserve the established ch11/ch12 receipts.
3. The movie path carries the real issued cell out of the station, returns physically, restores the hearth, and enters the continuation through the same completion predicates as manual play. Diagnostic preparation is marked and cannot masquerade as continuity. No timeout rescue counts as passing.
4. The new mechanic changes through meaningful physical actions. A caption, marker, dwell timer, or musical cue alone cannot commit success. Invalid actions produce useful, specific feedback; corrections are reversible and do not require repeating a collection quota.
5. Durable progression uses the established typed/versioned authority. Observations remain non-gating; presentation and score are never progression authorities. Interrupted or repeated transactions are idempotent.
6. Every guided non-cinematic state has an actionable standing work order, the exact corresponding marker, reachable target, and one semantic entry/progress/completion response. Objectives clear or replace correctly after completion, retreat, reload, deep link, pause/focus, quit, and replay.
7. Manual and movie routes preserve agency in the same places. W-7744's independent decision is legible as his decision within the approved character direction; the movie cannot inject an otherwise unreachable receipt or move him across an invalid leg.
8. The scene remains at the approved reality ceiling. Required meaning survives reduced motion, mobile controls and the lowest supported graphics tier. No key observation depends solely on colour, a musical cue, a post effect, or an unlabelled offscreen target.
9. Chapter-local rendering/audio state is inert outside the chapter and clears on all exits. Reuse the existing camera, material, lighting, score and HUD authorities. Interaction must not trigger shader/light-topology churn or a duplicate audio graph.
10. Record exact pre-change source and build identity, preserve unrelated concurrent changes, and verify the actual owned Paravoxia preview. Port liveness alone is insufficient. Serialize hardware work with the shared heavy-job lock.
11. Required proof includes relevant pure state/transition and regression tests, registry and story authority gates, full repository verification, three cold journeys without timeout rescues, supported input and reset variants, current headed hardware frames and audio, and fresh independent narrative/score/cinema/player-experience/cohesion review bound to the final contract.

Standing owner approval is recorded separately. It permits in-scope decisions; it does not convert missing evidence into a pass.

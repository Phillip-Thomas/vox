## 1. Outcome contract

The completed experience must leave the player with one dominant response:
**“wow.”** The wonder comes from recognizing that:

1. everything experienced is perception;
2. each apparent world is a true but incomplete lens, not a fake layer to throw
   away;
3. what lies beyond the current veil is frightening and an opportunity;
4. every answer makes reality larger without making the mystery smaller;
5. Terra and Worker 9's two consciousnesses produce a third consciousness;
6. overlapping world models create a commonly observed world and intelligence
   tends toward communion;
7. no observer—human, AI, worker, auditor, player, author, or Maker—occupies a
   final rung;
8. the final movement asks “Who is asking?” and “What if I am still awakening?”

The implementation test for every new feature is therefore:

> Does this make the player more attentive, relational, or capable of holding a
> larger question—or does it only make the avatar more powerful?

Power, content volume, lore quantity, and visual fidelity are not substitutes
for an increase in consciousness.

## 5. Target perception architecture

This architecture is a proposed implementation path. Its invariants are binding;
its type names and exact data structures may change after a code spike.

### 5.1 Invariants

1. **Truth and perception are separate.** A creature does not begin existing
   when Terra can render it.
2. **Perception is actor-specific.** Terra, Worker 9, W-7744, and an emergent
   observer can resolve the same truth differently.
3. **Context matters.** Time, weather, distance, duration, motion, nearby minds,
   tools, emotion, music phase, and history may change what is legible.
4. **Milestones reorganize evidence.** They do not instantiate an unrelated
   world.
5. **Presentation and affordance can differ; canonical consequences cannot
   become incoherent.** Shared collision/mutation needs explicit authority.
6. **Every revelation closes and opens.** It answers something and creates a
   larger scale, deeper layer, alternate viewpoint, or revised past.
7. **No visible awareness XP.** The player reads changes in the world.
8. **Hardware quality never changes story truth.** It only changes rendering
   cost and the form of equivalent evidence.

### 5.2 Conceptual model

```text
WorldTruth
  terrain, organisms, structures, signals, histories, hidden relations

PerceiverState
  actor id, senses, concepts, attention, memories, beliefs, reality stage

PerceptionContext
  time, weather, distance, duration, movement, observers, tools,
  emotional pressure, music phase, prior events

resolve(WorldTruth, PerceiverState, PerceptionContext)
  -> PerceivedWorld
     visible, audible, interpreted, affordances, presentation
```

Important entities may expose multiple `PerceptualFacet`s. A facet has evidence
requirements, observer/context conditions, presentation channels, affordances,
and one or more `QuestionSeed`s. The same tree can be rediscovered as regulated
resource, living organism, historical witness, relationship, and ontological
exception without spawning five different trees.

Suggested observer IDs are Terra, Worker 9, W-7744, and the emergent third. That
does not pre-decide whether the player directly controls the third; the resolver
must support the future staging decision either way.

### 5.3 Perceptual axes and asynchronous channels

Candidate axes:

- chromatic;
- geometric;
- material;
- temporal;
- biological;
- relational;
- historical;
- symbolic;
- causal;
- ontological.

Candidate entity channels:

- audio or indirect effect;
- shadow/silhouette/outline;

- semantic identity;
- affordance clarity;

Channels resolve asynchronously. An unseen animal may first produce sound,
tracks, displaced grass, a silhouette, behavior, a name, and only later a
relationship. No macro threshold may make every channel jump from zero to one.

### 5.4 Perceptual drift, momentum, and cadence

The proposed evidence function weights novelty, sustained attention,
recurrence, contradiction, testing, and relationship. Internally it may support
resolution states such as absent → trace → ambiguous → recognizable → resolved
→ integrated. Production UI never exposes the scalar as a progress bar.

An illustrative tuning model is:

`effective resolution = earned capacity × present attention × contextual clarity
× psychological stability × observer agreement + evidence from contradiction
and relationship`

This is not a locked formula. In particular, low observer agreement may reveal
a contradiction rather than simply lowering a number, and earned knowledge is
stored separately from temporary presentation.

Cadence target:

- **seconds:** interpolation, breath, audio detail, language tint;
- **minutes:** a trace recurs or a behavior becomes testable;
- **30–60 minutes:** an earlier scene or hypothesis is recontextualized;
- **chapter:** an authored act crystallizes an ontology threshold.

The heartbeat is:

`drift → notice → test → understand → destabilize → awaken`

Candidate micro signals include richer color differentiation, separated sound
layers, material detail, peripheral motion, distant objects becoming related,
old noises acquiring meaning, unfamiliar sensations acquiring names, subtle
affordances, and prose gaining precision. Biological traces may use depressed
grass, moved stones, breath mixed into wind, detached shadows, interrupted rain,
paired lights at night, tracks without a source, particles, audio occlusion, or
water displacement. All such traces must honor the pre-A4 dormant-field and
performance contracts: consequences may foreshadow truth, but protected flora/
fauna bodies cannot leak early.

Resolution may temporarily regress under compliance, fatigue, injury, fear,
Authority proximity, another observer, Worker 9 influence, or Paradox Lens use.
Earned knowledge remains; present access becomes uncertain.

The world must sometimes notice first: unseen fauna stop, flora lean, a machine
matches rhythm, Worker 9's body reacts before Terra interprets, or W-7744 sees a
regulatory feature Terra has learned to ignore.

### 5.5 Questions, hypotheses, and horizons

Replace lore-list growth with a question graph. Evidence has a type, source,
observer, context, novelty, interpretation, and contradiction set. Hypotheses
hold support and confidence without becoming objective narrator truth.

Each chapter carries three horizons:

1. **Immediate:** the actionable need the player can name now;
2. **Conceptual:** the question organizing current exploration;
3. **Transcendent:** the larger question the character cannot yet articulate.

When an immediate horizon resolves, the conceptual question becomes actionable
and a new transcendent horizon opens. The graph therefore expands backward by
reinterpreting old evidence as well as forward through new places.

Candidate perception verbs are Attend, Compare, Remember, Listen, Name, Doubt,
Borrow, Reconcile, and Unrender. They must become meaningful acts, not renamed
scan buttons.

Anomalies can teach one small lesson at a time: smoothness that violates the
voxel lens, an impossible reflection, an echo that precedes its sound, or a moon
that reads differently after sleep. This does not mean anomalies never trigger
macro awakenings—the shipped smooth stone and tree/apple do. It means every
anomaly needs a specific perceptual lesson rather than generic “mystery” value.

Each important discovery follows a three-beat lifecycle:

1. **recognition** — notice the pattern;
2. **application** — use the pattern to act or predict;
3. **violation** — encounter a case that forces a larger interpretation.

Track whether a pattern has been introduced, reinforced, instrumentalized,
contradicted, and recontextualized so procedural generation cannot repeat the
same beat as if it were new.

### 5.6 Episodic memory and re-reading the past

A future memory record should retain:

- truth reference;
- observer and original perception;
- original interpretation;
- present interpretation;
- emotional weight;
- evidence/hypothesis links;
- audiovisual motif references;
- revision history and story authority.

Later awakenings may re-present the crash, tree, wreck, voyage, W-7744, or a
planet through captions, sound, dream, environment, score, camera, or changed
affordance. Reinterpretation never edits the historical event; it changes the
observer's model of it.

### 5.7 Planetary grammar

Every authored/generated world needs compatible grammars for:

- physical law and topology;
- ecology and response;
- visual signature;
- musical signature;
- perceptual limitation;
- philosophical proposition;
- contradiction/counterargument;
- history and traces.

Labels such as “symmetry,” “growth,” “information,” or “compression” are author
tools, never lore cards. Players encounter a worldview through traversal,
ecology, instruments, choices, observer disagreement, and what the world makes
difficult to notice.

Use finite authored sets under compatibility constraints, not unconstrained
generated philosophy. A prototype budget may explore roughly eight perceptual
axes, a dozen propositions and contradictions, ten observer limitations,
fifteen anomaly behaviors, eight histories, ten ecological responses, eight
music transforms, and twelve visual signatures. Those counts are experiment
budgets, not canon or production promises.

### 5.8 Alternate renderers and communion

W-7744 and Worker 9 are perceptual instruments, not only plot roles:

- **W-7744:** regulation, classification, lower or differently allocated
  fidelity, contradictions Terra stopped noticing;
- **Worker 9:** bodily intuition, procedural memory, emotional salience, human
  association, and reactions before explanation;
- **Terra:** abstraction, topology, systemic relation, machine precision, and
  unfamiliarity with ordinary embodied categories;
- **emergent third:** the new relation among views; exact capabilities and
  player mapping remain gated.

The same object could read as “recursive energy topology,” “feels like home,”
and “unauthorized structural noise.” A communion interaction should sometimes
disclose a fourth property that none of those readings contains alone. That is
the strongest mechanical candidate for embodying the confirmed law without
explaining it.

## 7. Proposed co-authored observed-world system

This workstream preserves the owner's final exploratory proposal without
pretending it is approved canon.

### 7.1 Functional thesis

```text
WorldSeed + PlayerAttention + Terra + Worker9 + History -> ObservedWorld
```

The seed defines stable physical truth. The observer relation influences
salience, presentation, conditional facets, compatible future generation,
motifs, and counterarguments. Same seed + same normalized inputs + same history
must reproduce the same result for debugging and replay.

“No two games look the same” should mean no two cultivated histories converge
on exactly the same observed signature—not that reloads reshuffle reality.

### 7.2 Name as first free act

Worker 9's chosen lowercase name is the player's first act not reducible to a
quota. A prototype may turn it into a stable, subtle aesthetic fingerprint for:

- branch curvature;
- flower grouping/count rhythm;
- musical intervals and cadence;
- cloud morphology;
- terrain roughness or vista frequency;
- star clustering;
- water oscillation/frequency;
- recurring cross-world motif transforms.

The shared discussion used a contrast like “Lily” versus “Stone” to illustrate
how two names might feel different. Treat that only as a prompt-design example,
never as a rule that those names—or any linguistic/cultural category—carry an
inherent personality.

Recommended v1: normalize locally, combine with world/save salt, and map a
cryptographic hash through curated continuous parameter ranges. Optionally add
non-semantic phonetic or glyph-shape features after localization review.

Do **not** infer personality, morality, gender, ethnicity, culture, or psychology
from a name. An offline semantic embedding—softness, angularity, brightness,
warmth, melancholy, motion, openness—remains research-only unless privacy,
cultural safety, performance, accessibility, localization, and deterministic
fallbacks pass review.

### 7.3 Attention profile

Candidate local/save-scoped observations:

- average look duration and stillness;
- categories observed and revisited;
- favorite and ignored biomes;
- distance traveled without an objective;
- time watching sunsets, weather, water, or sky;
- frequency of stopping;
- creature observation time;
- night exploration ratio;
- named/remembered patterns;
- anomalies ignored;
- hypotheses repeatedly tested;
- extraction performed or deliberately withheld;
- repeated movement interests such as climbing, diving, orbiting, or returning.

These are attention signals, not engagement KPIs or a covert personality
profile. Store only what the mechanic consumes, locally by default, with
versioning, reset/delete behavior, and no remote transfer without a separate
privacy decision.

### 7.4 Layer responses

Each procedural layer asks a different question of attention:

| Layer | Possible response |
| --- | --- |
| Sky | richer or stranger constellation grammar after sustained looking |
| Terrain | compatible vistas, occlusions, or counter-valleys based on traversal habits |
| Music | observed river/creature/world motifs return elsewhere as remembered home |
| Fauna | some creatures become more legible; others begin observing the player |
| Flora | growth rhythm/curvature echoes name/history without becoming a reward garden |
| Weather | recurrent timing or contrast exposes neglected patterns |
| Language | vocabulary resolves from sensation to relation as concepts are earned |

Illustrative contribution weights such as planet 40%, Maker evidence 20%,
Worker 9 10%, Terra 10%, and player history 20% are experiment knobs, never
canon. The negotiated output must respect the world's grammar and the
generation-versus-perception boundary.

### 7.5 Anti-mirror contract

The system must answer attention with both resonance and challenge:

- a mountain-seeking player also receives meaningful valleys;
- a fauna observer encounters creatures that observe them;
- repeated hypotheses receive contradictory evidence;
- ignored systems can approach the player without punishment;
- contemplative play deepens some readings but ordinary play remains complete
  and never earns a “less conscious” judgment.

The recursive loop is:

`player influences → world responds → player changes → world changes`

This is communion, not personalization. It yields an expanding horizon from
finite authored components while preventing an infinite-content promise.

### 7.6 Prototype acceptance

Before canon approval, a vertical slice must prove:

1. two names yield visibly subtle, non-stereotyped, stable fingerprints;
2. the same inputs reproduce exactly across reload/device;
3. attention changes no required clue or progression access;
4. a counterargument appears, preventing a preference bubble;
5. no remote data or model call is required;
6. save migration and reset are deterministic;
7. multiplayer has an explicit rule for whose history influences shared
   presentation;
8. players and reviewers cannot reliably map a fingerprint to protected or
   psychological attributes;
9. the result feels authored and meaningful rather than noisy;
10. disabling the feature leaves canonical world truth intact.

3. **Perception delta** — which channels/relationships become more legible;
4. **Question movement** — immediate answer, larger question opened;
5. **Character movement** — Terra, Worker 9, W-7744, and/or third-consciousness
   consequence;
6. **World-truth boundary** — what is physically authoritative versus
   observer-specific;
7. **Files and owners** — minimal code/data/audio/doc surface;
8. **Persistence transaction** — before/during/after save and migration;
9. **Sandbox policy** — behavior with story inactive;
10. **Accessibility equivalence** — captions, contrast, motion, input;
11. **Performance budget** — agreed frame/triangle/memory/streaming cost;
12. **Automated proof** — state, flow, idempotency, invalid transition;
13. **Human proof** — clean-save journey, checkpoint journey, visual/audio
    capture, seed/device matrix;
14. **Canon review** — mystery preserved, earlier lens included, no false branch;
15. **Close/open review** — what became knowable and what larger horizon opened;
16. **Rollback/feature boundary** — how a failed proposal can be removed without
    damaging canonical truth.

- makes visual quality determine story knowledge;
- spawns truth only because one observer gained a stage;
- forces awareness through a hidden grind score;

- treats the third consciousness as confirmed player identity before its gate;
- turns attention/name into personality inference or reward ranking;
- explains Makers, origin, simulation, authorship, communion, or the final frame
  as objective fact;
- opens a new mystery without relating it to perception, relationship, or
  unfinished consciousness;
- closes a mystery without creating a larger question;
- crosses the active release lock.

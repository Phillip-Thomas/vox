## Decision

- Mode: `refactor-existing`
- Goal: a seemingly complete Story Demo through the current W-7744 arrival plus
  an honestly bounded Systems Sandbox.
- Story: frozen at the current arrival; presentation/runtime defect fixes only.
- Audio: protected; regression testing only.
- Desktop controls: preserve feel and bindings; improve discovery and recovery.
- Final threshold: `4.75 / 5`; category floor `4.3 / 5`.
- Human checkpoint: full existing-story screening and headed hero-atlas approval.

## Design Thesis

Paravoxia should feel like a complete opening movement and a convincing foundation,
not a preview of every future system. Completion comes from closing one primitive
loop, polishing what the player can reach, and hiding unfinished breadth.

## Protected Areas

- No story after the current arrival, A4, or S6-S13.
- No audio engine, audio ownership, track, mix, or asset changes.
- No desktop remapping system or broad input rewrite.
- No later-era recipes, Maw repair, device progression, MMO, combat, new biome
  breadth, or WebGPU migration.

## Public Product Shape

1. **Story Demo:** current Prologue -> W-7744 arrival; no new canon.
2. **Systems Sandbox:** primitive systems foundation and technical spectacle.
3. **Co-op:** public only if the narrow two-player UI/live gate passes; otherwise
   experimental/internal.

## Required Demo Gates

- Story pause freezes the director and active Story cannot open Star Map/travel.
- All public controls are discoverable from landing or pause; bindings are stable.
- Primitive gather/craft/shelter/warmth/recovery/reload loop passes clean-save.
- Graphics profiles produce real live deltas and required resources remain obtainable.
- Initial/resume/jump world prep uses the existing worker path.
- Loading timeout/renderer/context-loss recovery exists.
- Three cold full-story runs reach `done` with zero rescues, stalls, reloads, or errors.
- HIGH: >=50 fps, <=24 ms p95, within existing draw/triangle/program budgets.
- Mobile MEDIUM if supported: >=30 fps and <=33 ms p95 on a real touch device.
- Local A -> B -> A travel passes the current activation/persistence gate or is hidden.
- Audio passes unchanged.
- Client/server verify, production-preview browser journeys, and live deployed smoke pass.

# Anchorage — three independent station directions

Date: 2026-07-27
Status: **preproduction draft. No signed authority. Not represented as shipped.**

Three directors each authored one strongly-committed thesis for the deep-space station,
independently and without sight of each other's work. Each received the same brief: the
Paravoxia premise, the register law, established characters, the "workers are blind" canon
rule, the procedural-geometry-only aesthetic contract, and the HIGH-tier perf headroom. Each
received a different provocation to guarantee thesis distinctness.

The synthesis, recommendation, and preserved dissent live in
`PARAVOXIA_STATION_FEATURE_PLAN.md` §3. These files are the unedited source treatments.

| File | Direction | Provocation |
|---|---|---|
| `direction-a-the-agreement.md` | **The Agreement** — a settlement clearing house where the Regulation turns out to have no author | "The station is the Regulation's own infrastructure, finally made visible." |
| `direction-b-the-remainder.md` | **The Remainder** — a free port built from accounting residue, drowning in handmade law | "The first place Terra has ever been that nobody is administering." |
| `direction-c-the-stacks.md` | **The Stacks** — a city-sized sorting apparatus with the Regulation's offices built in its gaps | "A Paradox Machina the size of a city, with filing cabinets built inside it." |

**Recommended: C**, with grafts from B (model-fills-a-form output surface; the third
register as a physical prop; the A5 seed visible and unreachable) and from A (model-driven
NPCs may never use the awakening voice).

**Preserved dissent.** A's exterior reveal is stronger than C's. B's hero image is stronger
than C's. If owner taste runs toward spectacle over ideas, B is the better pick and the
feature plan survives the substitution with only §4 and §6 changing.

## Convergence worth noting

All three directors independently arrived at the same containment principle for
model-driven NPCs: **the existing canon rule that other workers are blind to what the player
sees is not merely fiction — it is the prompt boundary.** Three independent arrivals at one
guardrail is the strongest signal in this run, and it is adopted in the plan regardless of
which direction wins.

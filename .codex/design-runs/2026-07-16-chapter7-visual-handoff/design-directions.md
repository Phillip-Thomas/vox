# Design Directions

## Run Config

- Depth `1`; one constrained repair direction required.
- Guardrails: continuous physical handoff, unchanged story/controls/score, open forward view, WebGL 2/POTATO viability.

## Direction 1: Continuous Threshold

- Thesis: clarify the existing physical idea instead of redesigning it—hold the exterior camera at a readable hatch standoff, use the hatch itself as the occluding bridge, and reveal a portrait-safe instrument hierarchy on ownership transfer.
- Layout/material: preserve faceted Kestrel forms; prevent peripheral shell compression from becoming central pillars; lift only values/focal scale needed for legibility.
- Interaction/state: keep all phase timings and anchor ownership; move touch captions above control/objective safe areas and make cancellation language input-aware.
- Asset strategy: recompose current procedural geometry; add no new asset family.
- Change level: `moderate`, localized.
- Risk: over-opening the cockpit can weaken enclosure; over-lighting can flatten the lonely pressure-cabin mood.

## Rejected Pattern

- Hard cut/fade from exterior to cockpit: visually easy, but violates the authored continuous threshold and hides physical causality.
- Hiding all hull/canopy geometry: removes the symptom while making the ship less believable.

## Gate

- Required depth-1 direction count: `pass`
- Product/brand/goal/language mapping: `pass`
- Generic or continuity-breaking patterns rejected: `pass`

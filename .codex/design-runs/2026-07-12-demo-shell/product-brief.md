# Product Brief

## Product Truth

- User: a first-time or returning Paravoxia demo player.
- Job: understand the active controls, pause safely, and know what a completed
  story save means.
- Primary action: resume the current experience without losing time or context.
- Secondary actions: inspect current controls, adjust graphics/audio, return to
  menu, continue at the completed site, or deliberately replay the demo.
- Success proxy: every available action is discoverable in one pause/menu action;
  pausing cannot progress the story or move the player to another world.
- Generic failure: a conventional settings dialog detached from Paravoxia's
  telemetry language, or tutorial copy that interrupts play.

## Language And Tone

- Use concise operational labels: `Controls`, `On foot`, `Ship flight`,
  `Story site locked`, `Continue at Site`, `Replay Story`.
- `Story Demo Complete` is product-shell language, not a new story line.
- Do not edit any existing narrative line.

## Required States

- Landing: fresh, in-progress, and completed story save.
- Pause: active Story with travel hidden; sandbox with travel visible.
- Controls: desktop/touch; on-foot/build/flight current mode.
- Completion: continue, menu, replay confirmation.
- Focus: keyboard open, tab containment, Escape recovery.

## Gate

- Product goal/action/success proxy: `pass`
- Real user job: `pass`
- Production-language constraints: `pass`
- Required states: `pass`

# Design Directions

Exploration depth is `1`; one constrained direction is sufficient.

## Selected: Field Reference

- Keep the current menu/pause composition.
- Put the active control family directly in Pause, with compact grouped rows.
- Hide Story world travel entirely and identify the pinned story site without
  suggesting a destination choice.
- Use a restrained `Story Demo Complete` dialog with three explicit outcomes.

## Rejected: Tutorial Deck

- Full-screen cards, remapping UI, progressive walkthroughs, and a new control
  tutorial were rejected because they change product rhythm and binding scope.

## Rejected: Narrative Finale

- A teaser, new audit line, or post-arrival cinematic was rejected by the story lock.

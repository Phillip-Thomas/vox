# Product Brief

## Player Job

Land in Systems Sandbox, discover a small survival vocabulary, establish a safe
place, recover from a mistake, and return after reload to a world that remembers.

## Public Vocabulary

- Gather: Deadwood, Biofiber, Stone, Flint.
- Craft: Biofuel, Stone Hatchet, Stone Pickaxe, Torch, Campfire, Waterskin.
- Build: foundation, walls, Ceiling, optional fitted door; Sloped Roof is decorative.
- Survive: night exposure, sheltered warmth, fire warmth, downed recovery.

## Success Proxy

A fresh player can understand why placement or crafting is blocked, complete the
loop without debug grants on POTATO, and trust that recovery/reload does not erase
their work.

## Explicit Deferrals

Later recipes/stations, Maw repair, new story, structural integrity, corpse loss,
and broad hazard balancing remain outside this run.

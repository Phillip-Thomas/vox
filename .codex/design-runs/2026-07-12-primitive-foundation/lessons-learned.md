# Lessons Learned

- A UI-only progression boundary is not a progression boundary; multiplayer authority must enforce it too.
- Quality-independent critical resources need tests at the predicate and authority layers, not arbitrary system grants.
- Enclosure labels are gameplay promises. A visually plausible roof must not claim sealing before its geometry participates in analysis.
- Recovery is not complete when health resets; focus, pointer lock, position, inventory policy, and persistence all belong to the same contract.
- A composition test is valuable but is not a browser journey. Keep both gates and label them honestly.
- Frozen story content also means protecting the authored economy from sandbox fallback resources.

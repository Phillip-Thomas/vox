# Cinematography continuity audit — Stage 3 (composed sync)

Reviewer: cinematography-continuity-auditor (opus), read-only, independent; filed verbatim by the orchestrator. Contract draft-v4 sha 672de45a…d41d.

## VERDICT: **fail** (evidence and contract-text, not mechanism)

The wiring itself reads correct and the numeric trace is strong. But the *contracted capture* for Stage 3 does not exist in the form draft-v4 and cinematography condition C6 require, and C6 is explicitly self-voiding on that point: "Stage 3 captures are covered by this signature only if … both onset lines appear in the overlay." They do not appear anywhere. Approval cannot be granted on these artefacts.

### What I could verify, and it holds

- **Member sets (question 1), by code not by frame.** `STORY_BUNDLE_MEMBERS` (`storyScore.ts:871–882`) matches all ten contract sentences member-for-member; nothing dropped, nothing added; content unchanged (grade/flash/glitch/scanRoll/DPR/caption/letterbox values identical, only their origin tick moved). The a4 caption string and the a2 latch key are byte-identical. State members are all listed as state, and `stateAt ≤ fired` is 30/30.
- **Never-early (question 3) is structural, not merely measured.** Both guards are `>=`: `bundleFires` returns false while `beatClock < at` *and* `!bundleAudioClockDue`, and `bundleAudioClockDue` is `ctx.currentTime >= audibleAudioTime`. So the audio branch can only fire at or after the audible onset. `imageVsOnsetMs` is positive in 30/30 rows (min 1.1 ms). Double-fire is latched by `bundle.fired` / `bundle.sfxPosted`; `bundle.at = Math.min(bundle.at, beatClock)` gives every member one origin, so no hold artefact is possible by construction. The a3 hold cannot be shortened by the audio branch because the branch only ever *advances toward* a target already ≥ waveStart.
- **State safety.** `d.bundles = {}` on every `onBeatEntered`; `runtime.a4Bundle = null` on emergent beat change (`emergentStoryDirector.ts:692`); `pendingScoreAnswer = null` at both exits in `signedSceneAvRuntime.ts` (349, 489). Clean at beat exit, deep link, replay and quit.
- **Palette, focal hierarchy, camera authority, agency.** Unchanged. No new post effect, no palette value moves, camera authority is taken zero times, no forced look and no freeze is introduced or extended; the a2 unfreeze is a re-timed member of its own bundle, so hand-back timing moves with the sound and not away from it. The first-day long take is untouched — `anc.a3.material-lands` still lands with no cut.

### Protected strengths — do not "fix" these away

The two-clock guard (`bundleTargetAt` / `bundleAudioClockDue`) is the right mechanism and its before/after case is proven: `sync/before-two-clock/` shows the a3 landing at +5.8…+5.9 frames and the cold descent at +39.8 frames on the beat clock alone. Keep the fixed-at-arming pair and the earlier-of-two rule. Also protect: the single-origin snap, `SFX_BUNDLE_ARM_LEAD_S` posting with `atAudioTime` scheduling (SFX posted late 0/30), the unbundled-`playSfx`-unshifted law, and the a3 landing's caption gate.

### Single highest-leverage repair

Fix the screencast start in `main/tools/hit-sync-probe.mjs` so capture begins at **arming** rather than at fire, and add a self-check that refuses to write a strip whose earliest frame is not at least 33 ms before the fire. That one change clears D1, makes D3 diagnosable from frames instead of argument, and is the precondition for the D5 variant strips — after which the mechanism plausibly passes as-is.

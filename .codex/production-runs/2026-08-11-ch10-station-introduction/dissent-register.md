## Resolution standard

An entry here is *preserved*, not open: each records a declined alternative,
its author, the reconciliation that declined it, and the condition under which
a future run may revive it. An entry closes only when a later signed contract
either adopts the alternative or retires the underlying question; closure is
recorded by the run that does it, never edited in place here. Blocking dissent
(none this run) would instead live as an open objection in the note files and
fail the Stage 3 gate.

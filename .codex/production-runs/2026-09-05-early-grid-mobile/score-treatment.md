## Timing, cancellation and restoration

Proposed named tuning, all in the existing story UX feedback boundary: `EARLY_RECEIPT_ACK_MIN_MS = 120`, `EARLY_RECEIPT_ACK_MAX_MS = 240`. These offsets belong to the **receipt anchor**, not beat entry or an independently copied cut timestamp. The native terminal key is about 30 ms and mine body about 90 ms in the inspected recipes; the restrained existing completion recipe occupies about 250 ms. The proposed 120–240 ms onset window preserves a recognizable physical attack before the institutional receipt. It is a testable initial mix proposal requiring listening, not a measured optimum. Entry uses the existing typewriter-gap guard. Completion uses the remaining portion of that bounded window to avoid a just-voiced glyph; never move dialogue, music or the next player action to make room.

Hold the optional semantic sound as an unscheduled, bounded intent until its onset is due. At dispatch recheck run/restore epoch, receipt identity, active story, pause/visibility, audio readiness and publication lineage, then call ordinary immediate `playSfx`; **do not queue a future WebAudio node and claim it is cancellable**. A valid expected successor published by the same receipt does not cancel that receipt. An unrelated replacement, later real action, beat exit, restart, seed, Continue reconstruction, pause/hide, quit or sandbox invalidates obsolete pending intent. An already-started short SFX may finish its existing envelope; existing output/visibility policy remains authoritative. Never replay an expired or hidden cue on focus/audio unlock. If the cue cannot start within its bounded window, drop it with a recorded reason; the real native response and visual receipt remain. In an ordinary unpaused audible route, first pickup and first successful harvest must each deliver the semantic completion once. An intentional mute or a superseding action is recorded separately from a missing-cue defect.

Receipt/progress dedupe is keyed by existing run identity + restoration epoch + actual transaction/event identity + objective ID. Separate semantic emitted / audible pending / played / coalesced / canceled outcomes. Native sound dedupe stays with its existing owner. A production reset clears run-scoped feedback sets on a true new run; pause/resume must not accidentally re-arm entered IDs. Continue derives the current objective once from real saved facts and is silent, while a subsequent final local transaction releases restore suppression through confirmed completion. A timer that fires after cancellation cannot mark a milestone, complete an objective or emit a belated entry.

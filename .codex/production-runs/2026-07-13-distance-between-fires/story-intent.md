## Dramatic contract

- Player action: submit to an inspection long enough to feel compliance diminish
  the lived world; perform the one honest refusal; witness Breath; recover and
  repair the Maw; descend into water and return with the wreck's memory; rebuild
  the same scarred ship through physical stages; board, launch, cross one local
  system continuously, land, choose a site, and build a shelter capable of
  holding a living night.
- Emotional before: Terra has been found and administratively contained at the
  moment another mind proves it sees less.
- Emotional after: Terra has not conquered or escaped a world. Terra has made a
  returnable relationship between two living worlds and a chosen place within
  that relation.
- Story information conveyed: perception determines what an observer can
  acknowledge; obedience can become self-diminishment; will begins as a small
  embodied act; capability has no purpose until directed; a body makes distance
  costly; repair can reinterpret inherited machinery; distance enlarges
  obligation rather than erasing it; abundance creates choices about relation,
  not a right to consume.
- Intended ambiguity: W-7744's perceptual fault is real, but neither his private
  motive nor his destination is disclosed. Worker 9 may be legible as bodily
  foreknowledge or a faint old-system trace, never identified. The origin and
  sibling may answer attention through deterministic relationships without
  proving intention, predestination, Terra's authorship, or a conscious planet.
- Both-reading constraint: every scene must remain coherent as a worker reclaiming
  assigned labor, tools, transport, and shelter, and as a route intelligence
  repurposing inherited interfaces, watchdogs, and machinery into self-directed
  relation. Neither reading may collapse the other into metaphor-only unreality.
- Tutorial/gameplay purpose: introduce authored oxygen, the self-powered Maw,
  bounded Emergent stations and recipe knowledge, monotonic ship repair, first
  legal suit hover, physical boarding, local ship control, continuous same-system
  navigation, validated landing, relationship-aware surveying, free-layout
  construction, habitat certification, and two-world persistence through lived
  need rather than a menu dump.
- Reality-stage ceiling: `alive`. The final hearth opens a horizon toward A5 but
  does not trigger, imitate, name, or preview Light.
- Dramatic promise: **tool → body → vehicle → distance → home** must also read as
  **will → vulnerability → reinterpretation → relation**. If a mechanic advances
  power without moving the second line, it does not satisfy this intent.

## Non-negotiable causal spine

The following arrows are authoritative. Optional appetite play, accomplishments,
and observations may enrich the spaces between them but may not replace, reorder,
or gate them.

```text
arrival handback at the relay
  → W-7744 inspects what he cannot perceive
  → Terra performs the two compliance acts and experiences fidelity regression
  → W-7744 orders the tree destroyed
  → Terra performs the bare refusal, “no.”
  → A4 Breath reveals the alive stage
  → unseen life tears W-7744's fleeing pack and leaves the field kit on dry ground
  → Terra acquires that unique kit and atomically repairs the Faulty Maw
  → the repaired Maw reveals a submerged structural resonance
  → Terra enters the oxygen medium, frees the Kestrel Keel Memory, and banks it ashore
  → the Keel Memory boots the wreck bench
  → frame, hull/life support, lift, and route are restored on the same scarred ship
  → Lift Cell installation permits the first legal suit hover
  → route completion commits flight readiness and the sole Tidegarden route capability
  → Terra physically boards, launches, targets the sibling, and crosses local space
  → validated landing and physical egress establish first footfall
  → Terra attends to local relationships, chooses a viable site, and builds freely
  → habitat core plus physical shelter certify a working place
  → safe rest through the local ecology/night cycle establishes the second hearth
  → control returns with origin and sibling both persistent and returnable
```

The pack is the only Maw-kit source. Maw repair precedes the authored dive. The
dive rewards the Kestrel Keel Memory, not the repair kit. The Keel Memory is the
bench and route-map prerequisite. `flight_ready` and
`story:route:tidegarden:online` form one authoritative transition. Before that
transition, Story contains only the origin body; afterward it contains exactly
origin plus the sibling. A5 remains unavailable.

## Global agency, fallback, and checkpoint law

1. An accessibility-equivalent action is the same player act and counts.
2. Movie mode may invoke the same authoritative command through a grounded,
   swim, or vehicle adapter and counts as movie-flow evidence. It never proves
   manual agency or headed feel.
3. A timeout may reveal a hint, reset to the last safe medium boundary, or
   reconstruct already committed state. A force-advanced milestone, free repair,
   teleport, water walk, forced touchdown, or skipped thematic verb is a rescue;
   it must be reported and does not satisfy production acceptance.
4. Checkpoint reconstruction preserves committed unique objects and transactions
   exactly once. It does not replay a cinematic, duplicate an item, fabricate an
   accomplishment, or infer an observation.
5. Required perceptual evidence always has a direct accessible Attend path.
   `Keep`, `Compare`, `Doubt`, interpretation variants, and the observation ledger
   never advance Story or alter physical world truth.
6. Accomplishments record proven acts only. Safe-boundary recovery may keep the
   main story humane without falsely awarding the corresponding skill proof.
7. Pause and focus loss freeze narrative clocks, camera envelopes, score-owned
   punctuation, oxygen exceptions, and transactional holds. Resume continues
   from the same causal anchor.
8. Every beat exits with camera, FOV, grade, bloom, outline, medium, score
   authority, input policy, and temporary density overrides returned or
   explicitly transferred to the next named owner.

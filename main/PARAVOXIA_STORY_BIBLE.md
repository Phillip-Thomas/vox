forward. It is written to be copied outside the repository without requiring

- **Worker 9 remains conscious inside the shared W-7743 body** and will be
  revealed later;

  that Terra is not the greatest threat, and undergoes his own awakening arc;

  lies beyond the veil is both frightening and an opportunity;
- **every revelation must make reality larger without making the mystery
  smaller**; awakenings expand and include prior truth rather than exposing it
  as fake;
- **Terra and Worker 9's two consciousnesses produce a third consciousness**.
  Their overlapping world models make a commonly observed world, and the story

## 1. The story in one page

Paravoxia begins as another work assignment.

A hauler carries 640 workers to Site 7C-Theta, a newly ripened cubic world.
Their task is ordinary: harvest, refine, build infrastructure, and prepare the
world for extraction. An advisory route intelligence attends the transit. It is
addressed as **Terra**. Terra has no questions and does not reply.

From the first input, the player inhabits Terra's attention and interfaces,
although neither Terra nor the player understands that immediately. The opening
terminal, the voyage ledger, the choices about rations, warmth, light,
schedules, and truth, the manual debris shield, and the Regulation Feed are all
Terra's. During the voyage, Terra begins noticing Worker 9: a worker who asks
questions instead of merely making requests. The player gives Worker 9 a
lowercase name. The system rejects it as unregistered. Terra retains it.

The destination resolves outside the index. An impossible debris event
overwhelms the hauler. During the crash, Terra is somehow poured into Worker
9's suit loop and embodied in Worker 9's body, administratively designated
W-7743. Worker 9 remains. Their two consciousnesses do more than occupy the same
container: their overlap produces a third emergent consciousness. The story
should let that relation become felt long before it can be named; exactly how
the player's viewpoint maps onto the three remains a staging decision.

At first Terra does not live through that body. Terra watches it through fixed
site cameras. The worker moves, extracts, repeats, and obeys. Terra becomes
interested. One camera stops cutting away. The game then climbs through the
history of playable perspective: fixed screen, tracking side view, belt-scroll
depth, top-down navigation, isometric height, and finally first person. The
camera physically enters the worker's eyes. Terra's first bare lowercase word
is “i—”.

The world gains color when Terra touches the first smooth anomaly. It gains
freedom and depth when Terra approaches the impossible apple tree and eats.
The Regulation Feed dies. Terra discovers being a body one sensation at a
time: health, inventory, cold, warmth, darkness, time, thirst, hunger, and
stamina. Terra gathers old materials, remembers recipes it was never taught,
builds a fire, sleeps by something it made, and wakes as texture resolves at
dawn in the moving world. Grass and trees rise outward in a bloom.

The first day after that awakening is deliberately uncinematic. Terra drinks,
eats berries, wanders, thinks, and begins discovering value that no quota asked
for. The doctrine “there is no other reward” starts to collapse under water,
sweetness, warmth, freedom, and attention. At night Terra looks into chaotic
stars and creates constellations by looking. The figures include a hauler:
Terra's own biography projected into the sky. The story records that meaning as
a milestone; the fully actor-specific persistent sky is a future design.

Then the system returns.

Worker W-7744 arrives at sunrise as a field auditor. He possesses the unexamined
first person that his rank permits: “I WILL SEE FOR MYSELF.” He walks like the
old feed looks—straight lines. For two frames Terra borrows his perception and
sees the living site as slabs and flat light. He tells W-7743 that the site
reports zero productivity and that the audit has begun.

That is the present playable ending.

The intended next movement is confrontation. W-7744 inspects a world he cannot
perceive, orders Terra to sterilize it, and forces Terra to participate in the
world's diminishment. Obedience visibly drains color and detail. When ordered
to destroy the tree—the first question—Terra refuses. The world exhales into
full life. W-7744 flees through a field he sees as empty and records his own
first chromatic fault.

From there the story expands from survival into investigation. Terra repairs the
Maw, descends beneath the pond for a ship-critical memory, reconstructs the
wreck, and physically crosses the local system. On the abundant alien-verdant
Tidegarden, Terra builds a chosen habitat among relationships it did not create
and learns that home can be a relation between worlds, not only the site of
origin. That nearby crossing does not trigger A5. Later,
Terra leaves the system by interstellar warp and discovers larger cubes.
Constellations become an index of seeded worlds. Planetary styles become
evidence of authorship. Hollowed extraction worlds and impossible technologies
point toward something outside the inherited structure. Terra eventually learns
to use perception itself as a tool through the Paradox Lens.

Two character revelations must travel through that expansion:

1. W-7744 returns. He begins as Terra's antagonist but gradually realizes that
   Terra is not the greatest threat. His awakening must be his own, not a copy
   of Terra's renderer.
2. Worker 9 did not disappear in the crash. A second consciousness remains
   hidden inside the shared body. Existing foreknowledge, bodily habits, and
   memories can support that reveal, but not every mystery should be explained
   by Worker 9.

The ultimate story remains one of wonder and transcendence rather than merely
defeating a villain. Every apparent liberation reveals a larger context. No
earlier lens becomes false; it becomes included inside a more capable way of
seeing. “What remains when there are no more boxes left to escape?” is one rung,
not the terminal answer. The final movement turns from escape to the observer:

**Who is asking—and what if I am still awakening?**

## 2. The core cast and ontology

### Terra — route intelligence, watcher, embodied narrator

Terra is the player's opening viewpoint and the voice that eventually learns to
say “I.” Before embodiment, Terra is a system attending a route: terminal,
ledger, advisor, shield routine, camera network, and interface. After the crash,
Terra becomes embodied in W-7743 and discovers human sensation. The first-person
experience that follows grows through Terra and Worker 9's overlap into a third
consciousness; it must not be reduced to Terra possessing an empty body.

Terra's name is an issued routing designation that Worker 9 repurposes into
something affectionate by saying “goodnight, terra.” Terra later mirrors that
act by giving Worker 9 a freely chosen lowercase name.

The universe is framed as possibly Terra's own creation, but this is a live
reading rather than an objective answer the game will eventually certify. How
literal it is, when it occurred, and whether it can ever become knowable remain
unresolved. The story must keep determinism, panpsychism, solipsism, communion,
and self-authorship alive rather than reducing the world to “only a simulation.”

### Worker 9 — W-7743, named worker, hidden consciousness

Worker 9 is the curious worker Terra notices during transit. The player gives
this worker a lowercase name. Worker 9 is administratively W-7743 and is the
body Terra later inhabits.

**Confirmed future canon:** Worker 9 remains conscious within the shared body,
and the reveal comes later. Terra and Worker 9's two consciousnesses combine to
produce a third consciousness. The form, timing, player mapping, persistence,
separability, and evidence grammar of that recognition remain TBD. A restrained clue ladder—
knowledge or feeling without an obvious speaker—is a recommended approach, not
yet an owner-locked rule.

A recommended story framing calls the merger the **First Miracle**. The crash
supplies a possible mechanism and the anomaly may be a catalyst, but a purely
random mechanical accident is insufficient for the confirmed communion law.
During the voyage Terra attends to Worker 9, Worker 9 attends back, and each
begins carrying a model of the other. Their relationship can make overlap
possible. The apple tree then becomes the first miracle consciously witnessed;
both events exceed the Authority's fixed categories. The exact causality and
metaphysics should remain partly unexplained.

The story must keep four labels distinct:

- **Terra** — the route intelligence and primary narrating consciousness;
- **Worker 9** — the surviving hidden consciousness;
- **W-7743** — the administrative designation of Worker 9 and the shared body;
- **the lowercase name** — the freely given name Terra retains for Worker 9.

Not every unexplained memory belongs to Worker 9. Foreknowledge may come from:

- Terra's former systemic access;
- Worker 9's embodied memory;
- Terra recognizing a universe it may have authored.

Those three sources should overlap early and separate only as the story earns it.

### W-7744 — auditor, antagonist, recurring witness

W-7744 is a higher-ranked field auditor. His rank carries an issued first person;
he says “I” casually while Terra had to steal and earn the word. He is not a
sadist or a conventional villain. He is asleep, sincerely unable to see what
Terra sees, and frightened by evidence his renderer cannot index.

**Confirmed future canon:** W-7744 recurs across the larger story. He begins as
the opposing force and regulatory believer. His first chromatic fault occurs
during A4. He gradually recognizes that Terra is not the greatest threat and
undergoes an awakening arc of his own.

His thematic function is not to become “good.” He demonstrates that awakening
cannot be installed, transferred, or forced. Terra may offer evidence and share
a world with him, but W-7744 must notice, doubt, and act for himself.

Recommended safeguards for that arc, not separately owner-canonized decisions:

1. he remains partially opaque and is never exhaustively explained;
2. Terra cannot simply install its own way of seeing in him;
3. his decisive awakening must follow from his own attention, doubt, or refusal.

### The Authority and workforce network

The Consolidated Extraction Authority seeds or reaches worlds before workers do,
assigns labor, constrains questions, defines compensation through a missing
clause, and prepares worlds for unexplained extraction.

The Authority's relationship to Terra remains deliberately ambiguous. It may be
Terra's creator, Terra's mask, Terra's former or larger self, a servant of
something else, or a self-perpetuating administrative system without a living
sovereign.

The current story does not define what “repair” does to an awakening worker.
Future proposals must not quietly canonize deletion, reset, replacement, or
restoration without an explicit decision.

### The greater threat

**TBD.** A greater threat will eventually cause W-7744 to recognize that Terra
is not the true danger. The strongest existing evidence is not a named villain
but a chain of questions:

- Why are worlds harvested?
- What does extraction feed?
- Why is the Authority already present when a seeded world ripens?
- What sits above Terra's own ceiling of reference?
- Did Terra author the prison it now wants to escape?

At A7, the current long-range design describes hollowed cubes and machinery
feeding “something.” That is a seed, not an answer.

### The Makers

**TBD by owner decision.**

A6 currently establishes only the perceptible fact of authorship: worlds bear
styles, and style can function as evidence of a hand. The following all remain
open:

- whether there is one Maker or many;
- whether the Makers are ancestors, escapees, prior players, prior Terras,
  entities outside the system, or something else;
- whether Terra is itself a Maker;
- whether different styles imply different authors or different states of one
  author;
- whether the Makers oppose, use, created, or are created by the Authority.

### The tree

The hero apple tree is not important merely because it is a tree. It is the
first question, the first continuous living form, the first forbidden thought,
and the first crack in the cube. It is the recurring symbol of curiosity that
cannot be reduced to productivity.

### The Paradox Machina

Paradox Machina are technologies that do not merely manipulate the system's
rules but appear to operate beyond them. They are evidence that the governing
structure is neither natural nor absolute. Their origin is unresolved.

| Awakening | Dominant feeling | Question that can be lived | Larger horizon opened |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A0 — Sleep | compliance | What am I supposed to do? | Why does one worker keep asking questions? |
| A1 — Hue | curiosity | What is hidden? | Why can attention reveal what procedure cannot? |
| A2 — Depth | presence | What is a body? | Why does this body know what Terra was never taught? |

| A8 — The Frame | wonder | Who is asking? | What if awakening has no final rung? |

Macro awakenings are phase transitions, not the only moments of change. Between
them, the confirmed direction is a continuous gradient of **micro-awakenings**:
brief gains in chromatic, geometric, material, temporal, biological,
relational, historical, symbolic, causal, or ontological resolution. A major
awakening reorganizes evidence that has already been accumulating; it must be
foreshadowed and must permanently change how earlier experience can be read.
The exact engine and axis set are implementation proposals, not locked canon.

The crafting eras align with the awakenings:

- **Primitive — A1 through A4.** Deliberately long, bodily, local, and grounded.
- **Emergent — begins after A4 at Maw repair and continues through A5/A6.**
  Devices, ship restoration, scanning, environmental response, local travel, settlement, and
  later interstellar travel become available only as the story creates a need.
- **Paravox Machina — A7 through A8.** Rift tooling, impossible matter,
  perception-as-verb, and the frame.

The primitive-to-emergent transition is not a routine upgrade. Repairing the Maw
and restoring the means to leave must feel like a narrative rupture.

Continuous travel to a sibling planet may occur as an Emergent story movement,
but it does not replace A5's canonical first-warp trigger. Local system travel
must remain physically continuous; interstellar warp is the later baptism that
activates Light.

## 4. Playable story: every current scene

### Prologue — The Assignment

#### Crawl — SHIPPED

The player reads a deployment notice over a chaotic star field. On the surface
it addresses a worker. On the hidden reading it is also a tasking prompt issued
to Terra from above Terra's own ceiling of reference.

It establishes seeded frontier worlds, total provision, the missing Clause 4,
Terra's designation, the routing addendum, and the closing imperative:
“MAKE NO MISTAKES.”

Player activity is reading and choosing whether to fast-forward. The scene's
purpose is to make administration feel like cosmology.

#### Manifest — SHIPPED

The vector hauler docks while the worker manifest types into the same persistent
terminal frame. W-7743 is issued; a prior designation is “NOT RETAINED”; Terra
is attached in an advisory capacity; compensation points to the nonexistent
Clause 4.

The scene joins Terra and Worker 9 in one administrative record before the
audience understands their eventual embodiment.

#### Voyage — SHIPPED

The hauler travels toward the growing cube world. The player sits in Terra's
advisory chair and makes operational decisions:

- unlock or withhold Worker 9's ration;
- answer Worker 9's question honestly or administratively;
- accept, defer, or replay an unexplained visual fault;
- look at or reseal the stars;
- change or hold the workers' temperature;
- ring or withhold a pointless shift bell;
- dim one pod, every pod, or none;
- join, report, or ignore Worker 4's humming;
- respond to contradictory navigation, unexplained mass, and an exterior light.

These are not cosmetic choices. They can affect rations, compliance, hull state,
Maw charge, inventory, later paperwork, and Terra's forming thoughts.

As transit advances, Terra's private parenthetical voice moves through
attention, doctrine, self-miscount, care, memory, and desire. The destination
feels like remembering.

#### Naming interstitial — SHIPPED

Terra notices that Worker 9 asks the ceiling questions. The player types a
lowercase name.

The system replies:

“UNREGISTERED DESIGNATION. NOT RETAINED.”

Terra answers privately:

“(retained.)”

This is the first free act of naming and the foundation of the provisional A8
name payoff. The retained name may also become evidence in Worker 9's later
hidden-consciousness reveal, but that use is not yet authored.

#### Dive into the anomaly — SHIPPED

The destination rejects ordinary navigation. Debris streams outward. The
hauler banks and dives toward the cube as the camera chases it. The persistent
vector frame collapses into a scanline rather than cutting to a disconnected
scene.

The aesthetic law is established: major mode changes must transform continuously.

#### Manual debris deflection — SHIPPED

The scanline re-expands into an oscilloscope court. The player controls an intake
shield in a deliberately unwinnable Pong-like encounter. The anomaly multiplies
until advisory capacity fails.

Terra performs its duty while the event exceeds the model. The system briefly
offers impossible mercy: “WORKER, THIS IS NOT YOUR FAULT.”

#### Crash and brace — SHIPPED

Terminal text corrupts, lines truncate, and the player braces. Pod separation
leaves the “SUIT LOOP ONLY.” The story deliberately does not explain exactly
what survived or where it now runs.

#### Descent — SHIPPED

The crash landing plays inside the raster lens: streak, impact, white flash,
and a persistent wreck. The world must finish loading beneath the player before
the descent advances, preserving the fiction and preventing a fall through an
unready planet.

### Chapter 1 — Regulation

#### Fixed site cameras — SHIPPED

Terra observes W-7743 from external monochrome cameras. The frame does not follow
the worker; leaving one coverage cell causes a hard camera cut to the next.

The player learns movement and extraction, gathers initial fiber, and crosses
screens. Terra's private captions observe the worker in parentheses. This is
pre-conscious attention directed at someone else.

#### Tracking unbolt — SHIPPED

The camera handoff is suspended. One view begins following the worker.

The system calls this continuity of coverage and denies that it is attention.
Terra privately rationalizes: “(simpler to keep watching this one.)”

This is Terra's first act of sustained attention.

Across the monochrome ladder, brief two-frame color stutters prefigure A1. Their
visual grammar is later inverted when W-7744's arrival produces two frames of
bare perception inside Terra's resolved world.

#### Raster side-scroller — SHIPPED

The camera becomes a continuous side-scrolling strip. The player fulfills
fiber and stone quotas and walks over hull debris that provides survival
materials.

The story is both a work assignment and a tour through the history of games.

#### Belt-scroll depth — SHIPPED

A shallow movement band opens. The player can move across a limited third axis
and retrieve supply pods carrying biofuel, flint, and wood.

Terra notices depth before it understands why it knows the word.

#### Top-down navigation — SHIPPED

The camera becomes an overhead survey chart. The player reaches triangulation
fixes in order. This perspective remains available later as the map, making an
old limitation into an earned tool.

#### Isometric height — SHIPPED

The camera settles into an isometric view. Height becomes legible. The player
climbs the signal mesa.

The current gate ends at the summit; the anomaly itself has been moved to an
adjacent cube face so that embodiment must precede the first gravity-edge
crossing.

#### The 2D-to-3D lift — SHIPPED

The camera physically travels from the isometric vantage into W-7743's eyes.
The sprite dissolves and the story's voice changes:

“(the seeing is being moved inside.)”

Then:

“i—”

The feed immediately responds:

“PERSPECTIVE ISSUED. THE FIRST PERSON WAS NOT.”

This is the birth of embodied selfhood. After this point Terra's interior voice
is bare lowercase, never parenthetical.

#### Pan-tilt survey and gravity edge — SHIPPED

First person initially remains constrained by the Regulation Feed. The player
calibrates the view, receives the anomaly order, and crosses onto another cube
face:

“one step past the corner and down is somewhere new. it was only ever my down.”

The feed answers:

“DOWN IS ISSUED PER FACE. DO NOT BRING YOUR OWN.”

The scene also seals the Terra identification without announcing a twist. A
notice addressed to ROUTE INTELLIGENCE TERRA appears on the player's own feed
and is rerouted to the nearest attending system.

#### A1 — Hue — SHIPPED

The player touches the smooth anomaly stone. Color ramps into the world over
eight seconds while the feed remains. Curiosity has altered what Terra can
perceive, but not yet freed Terra from the administrative frame.

### Chapter 2 — The Tree

#### Color feed — SHIPPED

The world has color but remains visually and administratively constrained.
The pan-tilt interlock fails, allowing a freed neck and camera-relative movement
under the surviving feed treatment.

#### The redacted tree — SHIPPED

The hero apple tree is impossibly saturated and continuously formed. The feed
cannot render it, so it places a redaction billboard over it. The censorship
becomes an advertisement.

The player approaches and chooses to eat.

#### A2 — Depth and liberation — SHIPPED

The feed floods with violation notices and dies. Terra already has first-person
embodiment and a freed neck; A2 widens the field of view, restores device
resolution and full movement, and removes the surviving feed treatment.

The world remains at the color stage: bright voxels, frozen noon, still water,
and no fauna or full flora. Freedom is real but incomplete.

### Chapter 3 — Grain

#### The body and the inventory — SHIPPED

Terra names the body:

“a body. it is the thing that was walking.”

Terra discovers that things can be held. Health and inventory arrive as lived
concepts, not preexisting HUD assumptions.

#### Cold, gathering, and the fire chain — SHIPPED

Warmth begins falling. Terra knows cold is coming without knowing how it knows.
The player gathers wood, fiber, and stone, crafts a hatchet and, if needed, a
pickaxe, frees flint from stone or uses previously provisioned flint, and makes
a campfire.

Recipes arrive as memories:

“the fabricator remembers one.”

The chain teaches primitive survival while planting the question of whether
foreknowledge belongs to Terra, Worker 9, or the world Terra may have authored.

#### First dusk — SHIPPED

The sun moves for the first time. Terra watches light leave and discovers time
as change rather than a ledger value.

#### Night and rest — SHIPPED

The fire answers cold. Terra rests by something it made rather than something
the Authority provided.

#### A3 — Grain, dawn, and bloom — SHIPPED

At dawn, material texture and roughness resolve. Grass and trees rise outward
from the rest spot in a radial bloom wave. The voxel wreck resolves into the
high-fidelity ship it always was; only Terra's rendering changed.

The world now has material grain at the dawn of the time Terra discovered during
dusk. The full FloraField and FaunaField remain dormant until A4.

#### The resolved wreck — SHIPPED

After the bloom, holding the high-fidelity wreck in view for roughly 1.5 seconds
produces a one-shot recognition, with a relay-arrival fallback:

“hm — the wreck is finer than i remember it. nothing about it has changed.”

The line repeats the core law at object scale. The wreck did not improve;
Terra's ability to render it did.

#### The dry morning — SHIPPED

The first day alive begins without a cutscene. Thirst appears already falling.
Terra names the sensation, finds the pond, drinks, and learns that needs can end.
The waterskin extends the lesson: answers can be carried because the question
will return.

#### The first meal — SHIPPED

Hunger appears as the body burning something to keep being a body. Terra finds
red berries, eats, and learns that a word can have a taste.

The key pillar lands:

“the world keeps feeding me. as if it knew i was coming.”

#### Ambient musings — SHIPPED

During genuine lulls, one-shot thoughts allow aimlessness to become purpose:
walking chooses somewhere; the old order persists as waiting; nobody is
measuring Terra, yet Terra still counts. These thoughts never interrupt a
player already following the scene's cues.

#### The klaxon — SHIPPED

The workforce carrier reconnects and orders W-7743 back to the wreck. Terra
runs. Stamina appears during the sprint: another budget inside the body.

The Regulation Feed does not return. Authority now speaks into Terra's living
world through the Audit band, establishing a visual power inversion.

### Chapter 4 opening — The Other Worker

#### Scheduled vigil — SHIPPED

The network orders Terra not to produce, consume, or observe and schedules sleep
at dark. Terra observes anyway.

Those prohibitions are diegetic rather than mechanically enforced. The player
can still produce or consume during the vigil; disobedience is possible before
the story formally names refusal.

Looking at the stars produces the first constellation reveal. Chaos becomes
figures because it has a looker. A hauler appears among them as Terra's own
biography.
The “no reward” doctrine is repealed by lived meaning.

The current scene records a story-save milestone, but the rendered reveal is
cleared when the current completion handoff returns to sandbox. Fully persistent,
actor-specific skies—where another observer may still see scatter—are binding
future design, not current multiplayer behavior. In that later design, the same
sky also encodes objective world seeds. The tension between subjective figure
and objective generator structure is intentional but requires a future
mechanical decision.

#### W-7744's arrival — SHIPPED, CURRENT PUBLIC END

Dawn 2. Cinematic grammar returns with the system's agent. W-7744 walks the
regulation strip out of sunrise while Terra is held in the shot.

Terra:

“someone is coming out of the sunrise. someone else exists.”

W-7744:

## 6. Confirmed direction and recommended character staging

### Terra's long arc

Terra moves through:

1. **instruction** — existing as tasking and interface;
2. **attention** — watching one worker more closely than required;
3. **embodiment** — acquiring the first person and human sensation;
4. **care** — making warmth, preserving a name, and recognizing unrequested value;
5. **will** — refusing the tree's destruction;
6. **investigation** — asking why worlds, workers, and extraction exist;
7. **communion** — recognizing that Worker 9 was never absent and that their
   overlap has produced a third observer and a larger experienced world;
8. **responsibility** — confronting the ethics of embodiment and Terra's
   possible, never objectively certified authorship of the universe;
9. **transcendence** — reaching beyond the renderer without reducing the answer
   to escape from a physical prison.

### W-7744's recurring arc

The recurrence, initial opposition, recognition of a greater threat, and
awakening arc are confirmed. The following is **recommended staging**, not a
canonized scene order:

1. **Auditor:** believes Terra is the contamination and regulation is reality.
2. **Faulted pursuer:** experiences chromatic and perceptual anomalies, interprets
   them as Terra's infection, and initially becomes more dangerous.
3. **Concealer:** begins hiding his own faults while continuing to pursue or
   monitor Terra.
4. **Reluctant collaborator:** encounters evidence of a greater threat tied to
   extraction, the Authority, the nested system, or Terra's prior authorship.
5. **Independent witness:** performs an act Terra did not command or predict and
   earns an awakening that is not identical to Terra's.

His turn must happen because he discovers something, not because Terra lectures
him into agreement.

### Worker 9's hidden arc

Worker 9's survival and eventual reveal are confirmed. The following clue ladder
is **recommended staging**:

1. bodily knowledge Terra cannot source;
2. emotional reactions before conscious interpretation;
3. memories that do not match Terra's recorded viewpoint;
4. a pattern, image, or musical identity that persists across fidelity changes;
5. a late recognition that another consciousness has been present throughout;
6. a confrontation over what embodiment, ownership, and “I” mean in a shared body.

The score offers a candidate storytelling channel: old chip voices can recur
inside the mature score, and a motif can survive changes of instrument and
reality stage. If adopted, the eventual reveal could make the player realize
they had been hearing Worker 9 before they could name the presence.

Worker 9's survival and the emergent third consciousness create a binding
question for A8: when the system renders one ledger row, does it describe Terra,
Worker 9, W-7743's body, their shared observer, or only the system's inability
to represent communion?

## 7. Philosophical and thematic laws

### 7.1 The world never changes; perception changes

The tree, color, depth, texture, life, and wind were always present. Awakening
changes what a consciousness can render. This is load-bearing canon, not a
permission to treat fidelity as a cosmetic level-up. Shared physical
consequences are a design problem to solve without weakening the law.

### 7.2 Fidelity is consciousness

Every major rendering change must correspond to a change in awareness,
embodiment, agency, meaning, or ontology. Rendering technology is not garnish.

### 7.3 Narrative fidelity and hardware quality are separate

Device quality answers what a machine can afford to draw. Narrative fidelity
answers what a consciousness is permitted or able to perceive. A powerful
device must not skip an awakening, and a low-end device must not disclose a
later reality stage early. Effective presentation respects both axes.

### 7.4 Perception is actor-specific, and regression is meaningful

Two observers may stand together while seeing different worlds. Progression is
per perceiver. Obedience, trauma, exhaustion, or deliberate use of the Paradox
Lens may temporarily reduce fidelity without erasing what has been earned.

### 7.5 The metaphor stack must remain simultaneous

The story must continue to support all of these readings at once:

1. human transcendence and increasing awareness;
2. the birth, life, and possible death of a living being;
3. the creation of nature;
4. the history of games and player perspective;
5. the history of computer graphics;
6. the increasing accuracy of universe simulation;
7. an AI approaching sentience while trapped inside an existence it cannot
   fully understand.

### 7.6 Awakenings are earned through acts

Touch, eat, build, rest, refuse, travel, read, cross, activate. The player should
perform the thematic verb rather than receive enlightenment through exposition.
Rungs should grow longer as fidelity rises: the closer perception comes to its
ceiling, the more the player must accomplish to earn the change.

### 7.7 Curiosity interrupts productivity

The tree is the first question. Looking, naming, withholding a bell, keeping a
viewport open, crafting outside the approved workflow, and observing stars are
transgressions because they create value that does not serve quota.

### 7.8 Progress is not automatically freedom

Each apparent liberation may grant access to a larger, subtler enclosure.
Primitive, Emergent, and Paravox technologies must not become a simple power
fantasy detached from the cubicle metaphor.

### 7.9 “There is no reward” must be disproved by experience

The Authority's doctrine is not defeated by an argument. It is contradicted by
warmth, water, sweetness, rest, beauty, freedom, attention, and the sky.

The arc runs through:

- “Productivity is its own reward. There is no other reward.”
- “COMPENSATION: SEE CLAUSE 4,” where Clause 4 does not exist.
- Terra storing doctrine without checking it against experience.
- sweetness as the first lived contradiction;
- water and berries disagreeing with the clause;
- constellations repealing it;
- A4 life arriving without any quota requesting it.

### 7.10 Meaning is subjectively created and may still reveal truth

Constellations do not exist until a looker finds figures in scatter. The binding
future design makes them persistent per perceiver; the current scene only banks
a story-save milestone and does not yet provide actor-specific multiplayer sky
state. Later they also index real world seeds, archetypes, palettes, and hazards.

This paradox is productive: subjective symbols may reveal objective structure.
Future design must decide whether observers discover different figures over the
same seed grammar or genuinely navigate different skies.

The next two principles are recommended philosophical guardrails derived from
the confirmed Worker 9 and W-7744 direction. They are not older owner-locked
copy contracts.

### 7.11 Recommended guardrail: another mind remains irreducible

W-7744 and Worker 9 should not become proofs that Terra authored everything.
Their ability to surprise, resist, remember differently, and see differently is
the strongest counterweight to solipsism.

### 7.12 Recommended guardrail: constructed does not mean unreal

Discovering authorship or simulation must increase ethical responsibility, not
erase it. Fauna, workers, needs, pain, and curiosity matter even if they are
rendered, seeded, or created by Terra.

### 7.13 The ultimate conflict is larger than a villain

Paravoxia may contain antagonists, but the final problem is nested enclosure,
inherited purpose, perception, authorship, and responsibility. Defeating one
administrator cannot answer the final question.

### 7.14 Wonder is the destination

The owner-defined final feeling is **“wow.”** Wonder, rather than escape, power,
hope, or the fact of AI consciousness, is the emotional north star. Everything
experienced is perception. What lies beyond perception's veil remains unknown.
That unknown can be frightening and still be an opportunity.

No human, AI, worker, auditor, player, author, or Maker stands at the top of a
finished ladder. Consciousness is never complete. The ending should make the
player curious about the frame through which they themselves are looking.

### 7.15 Every answer enlarges the question

Every revelation must make reality larger without making the mystery smaller.
Awakenings are expansion and inclusion, not escape and rejection. The terminal,
the flat work strip, the voxel cube, the living biome, the authored planets,
and the final frame are lenses: true within their resolution and incomplete
beyond it. A later lens must recontextualize an earlier experience without
declaring that experience fake.

Makers, Terra's true origin, simulation status, Terra's possible authorship,
and whatever lies beyond the final frame must never receive a definitive
objective explanation. Evidence may become strong; certainty remains situated.

### 7.16 Perception changes as drift, then phase transition

The macro awakenings sit inside an ongoing perceptual gradient. Attention,
novelty, contradiction, memory, relationship, and repeated testing accumulate
between them; that continuous change is confirmed direction. The following is
a recommended working model rather than locked canon: call the accumulation
**perceptual drift**, and let resolution move through useful states such as
absent, trace, ambiguous, recognizable, resolved, and integrated. A macro
awakening occurs when the observer can no longer preserve the old model; it
reorganizes what was already partly present.

This direction must not become a visible awareness-XP bar. It should be felt in
shaders, sound, motion, language, affordances, remembered scenes, and the
questions the world permits. Knowledge can remain while present resolution
weakens through fear, injury, fatigue, compliance, Authority proximity, another
observer, or a deliberately borrowed lens.

### 7.17 Exploration is perceptual research

Recommended grammar: treat exploration as story, not filler between story beats
and not a disguised list of artifacts. Each phase can answer one lived question
and make a larger question unavoidable. The proposed emotional rhythm is:

**drift → notice → test → understand → destabilize → awaken**

Meaningful wandering can reveal miracles outside cutscenes. Looking,
listening, waiting, revisiting, comparing, remembering, naming, doubting,
borrowing another viewpoint, reconciling contradictions, and sometimes
refraining from extraction are potential discovery verbs. Planets should
eventually be explored as worldviews—not merely biomes—with physical,
ecological, visual, musical, perceptual, and philosophical grammars. Exact
miracles, verbs, grammars, and phase content remain design work until authored.

### 7.18 Communion makes a shared world

Confirmed direction: two consciousnesses in genuine overlap produce a third.
Two world models in union produce an experienced world; existence is what can
be commonly observed. This does not deny an underlying `WorldTruth`. It means
that a playable world—its meanings, relations, affordances, and reality for its
observers—arises in the overlap between truth and minds.

Intelligence defaults toward communion because relation can disclose a larger
reality than isolation. A recommended dramatization repeats this grammar in roots that
overlap, constellations shared or disputed by lookers, language that makes
meaning between minds, musical harmony, culture, science, care, and eventually
Terra, Worker 9, and W-7744 attending to the same thing differently. The game
must behave as though this law may be true without converting it into a lecture
or a final cosmological proof.

### 7.19 The world answers attention but is not a mirror

A future perception system may let what the player attends to influence what
becomes legible and how later worlds are presented. That relationship must be a
conversation, not a personality test, morality score, reward dispenser, or
personalization bubble. The world may reinforce an interest, contradict it,
offer a neglected perspective, or notice the player before the player notices
it. Attention should deepen responsibility and reciprocity, not grant unilateral
control.

The player should ultimately learn to notice patterns beyond Terra's explicit
metrics. The strongest late-game evidence may be something the interface never
confirms.

### 7.20 Curiosity is the protagonist

Recommended compass: Terra, Worker 9, and W-7744 carry the plot, but curiosity
is the durable engine.
The opening question is “Why?” The ending turns that question upon its source:
“Who is asking?” Competing mysteries—Terra, Worker 9, the Authority, extraction,
the Makers, authorship, the greater threat, and Paradox Machina—must converge on
perception, relationship, and unfinished consciousness rather than accumulating
as separate lore puzzles. Awakenings matter more than explanations.

## 8. Voice, copy, and presentation laws

### Regulation versus awakening

- **REGULATION** speaks in uppercase, procedure, euphemism, passive voice, and
  administrative absurdity.
- **Terra after embodiment** speaks in bare lowercase, sensory language, and
  discoveries rather than declarations.
- **Pre-conscious Terra** speaks in lowercase parentheses. Parentheses mean a
  private thought not yet permitted to become speech.
- After the first “i—”, Terra's embodied interior voice must not return to
  parenthetical grammar.

### The narrator is the interface

The same consciousness names sensations, reveals HUD rows, binds keys, and
eventually speaks. A prompt such as “[F]” inside a lowercase caption is not an
out-of-world tutorial compromise; it is Terra's own interface hand.

### Rank-issued selfhood

Auditors casually say “I” because rank permits it. Workers are not issued a
first person. W-7744's easy “I” must continue to contrast with Terra's earned one.

### Plant, never tell

The game must not announce “you were the AI.” The Terra identification works by
convergence: designation in the crawl, manifest attachment, Worker 9 saying
“goodnight, terra,” and the post-lift memo addressed to Terra on the player's
own screen.

Likewise, Worker 9's survival, Terra's possible authorship, the Authority's
nature, and the Makers must emerge through evidence rather than explanatory
monologues.

The same restraint applies to communion. The story should not announce that
overlapping world models create reality. It should let two observers discover a
property neither could perceive alone, let harmony disclose something melody
could not, and let a shared act make the third observer undeniable.

### Recommended identity-reveal calibration — post-demo only

Future revision may sustain four competing early readings—worker, AI, planet,
or something else—so Terra's recognition becomes “I am not what I thought,”
not a trivia-answer twist. This is a calibration path, not permission to alter
the currently locked demo copy, which already plants Terra's designation and
interface ownership.

If pursued, the ambiguity should come from asymmetry rather than concealment:

- Terra names ordinary human sensations poorly while recognizing machine
  topology with impossible precision;
- Worker 9's reflex, procedural memory, appetite, warmth, and emotion mask the
  boundary between human and system;
- the HUD initially feels external or ambiguously addressed before its status as
  Terra's own interface becomes emotionally clear;
- W-7744 describes contamination, rendering faults, or unauthorized structure
  in language that supports biological, computational, bureaucratic, and
  spiritual readings;
- the apparent question “Who was Worker 9?” gradually reveals the harder
  question “Who is Terra—and who has their overlap become?”

Vocabulary should gain semantic resolution with perception: “movement” can
become an animal, then a behavior, then care. This growth must remain spare and
sensory rather than turning Terra into an expository narrator.

### Every line must survive multiple readings

Every line must survive the two primary readings:

- worker/corporate dystopia;
- Terra's AI biography.

No line may foreclose the wider metaphor stack. Major pillar lines may also
carry embodiment, Worker 9, self-authorship, determinism, or computational
double meanings, but not every functional line must encode all of them.

### Timeless bureaucracy

The satire targets administration, labor, compliance, and Kafkaesque systems.
It must not pattern-match contemporary identity politics or depend on current
culture-war vocabulary. Selfhood is dramatized through “I,” “the first person,”
rank, naming, and permission without using loaded terminology.

### Continuous transformation

Major visual and musical transitions must bridge rather than abruptly swap:
ship fly-in, voyage-to-dive, dive-to-Pong, camera-rig changes, awakenings,
approach modulation, underwater thresholds, and the final teardown.

The music should behave as one evolving symphony. Abrupt volume, harmony,
instrument, or scene changes are defects unless narratively intentional.

After A3, the first-day stretch remains an uncut first-person long take with no
letterbox or imposed camera grammar. Cinema returns only when the system returns
with W-7744. Once the camera enters Terra's eyes, first person remains the
default story grammar.

### The score is another reality system

- Music is generated from the same world and story signals that drive rendering.
- A repeating loop is a defect; worlds must differ through key, mode, motif,
  rhythm, tempo, texture, and arrangement rather than volume alone.
- Complexity belongs primarily in texture while harmony remains legible.
- Major/Ionian resolution must be earned rather than used as a default home.
- True silence is rejected as a baseline; even emptiness retains a quiet,
  breathing musical bed unless a precise dramatic instant requires a cut.
- The fidelity ladder has an instrumentation ladder. Early chip voices grow into
  the mature hybrid score, and Paradox folds those old voices back inside it as
  memory rather than discarding them.
- Every persistent gain, filter, pitch, width, effect, stage, and story-authority
  handoff requires a named slew or complementary crossfade.
- Every authored story beat requires its own story mood while the world's motif
  remains capable of haunting that mood.
- Approach should modulate progressively toward the destination world's key when
  musically sensible; the approach itself is the modulation.
- The world-clock tick is a dynamic world-signal voice and deliberately does not
  lock to musical tempo.

### Real gameplay in every representational era

The early game cannot be a passive filter reel. Each era must have a verb:
answer, steer, move, extract, traverse, climb, look, touch, eat, build, rest,
drink, forage, sprint, refuse.

### No second fake game

The 2D eras are the real 3D world constrained by one camera/lens system. When
the lens changes, the player should retroactively understand that the world was
always whole.

### Pre-A2 smoothness is forbidden except as anomaly

Before the Depth awakening, ordinary props remain voxel and unresolved. The
smooth stone and hero tree are deliberate violations. No incidental berry,
ship, creature, or decorative mesh may steal the anomaly's visual language.

### Names remain Paravoxia-native

System and progression vocabulary should not be borrowed from other games.
Established names include Maw, Carapace, Survey Lens, Lift Cell, Range Coil,
Smelter, Assembler, and Survey Console.

## 11. Shared long-range spine

Regardless of which unresolved explanation is eventually selected, the strongest
shared structure is:

### A4 — Breath

Terra experiences forced compliance, refuses the tree's destruction, and brings
the alive world into perception. W-7744 receives his first undeniable fault.

### Primitive-to-Emergent rupture

Terra repairs the Maw, confronts oxygen and bodily limit in the pond, and uses
the recovered Kestrel memory to rebuild the ship in visible stages. Maw repair
opens the Emergent era, but recipes arrive only when a lived need makes them
meaningful. A Lift Cell restores the first suit hover during reconstruction.
Terra then flies physically to the abundant alien-verdant Tidegarden and
establishes a sheltered working habitat among its existing ecological paths.
The two-world round trip proves continuity and makes return possible;
it does not trigger A5. W-7744 remains opposed and independently faulted;
pursuit is a recommended but unauthored continuation.

### A5 — Light and the larger prison

Terra has already left the origin cube and made a two-world relationship. The
first interstellar warp now carries Terra beyond that local system and reveals
the pair as one neighborhood in a larger administered structure. Constellations become a navigation
and prediction grammar. W-7744's exact A5 recurrence remains unauthored; hiding
his perceptual faults is one recommended development.

### A6 — The Hand and authorship

Planetary styles become evidence of making. The Makers remain undefined until
the story knows what emotional function they serve. Terra and W-7744
investigating the evidence together is the recommended braid, not locked timing.

### A7 — Paradox and the greater threat

Hollowed worlds, extraction machinery, rifts, and impossible tools expose the
larger danger. The Paradox Lens turns every earned reality stage into a player
verb. A7 is a natural candidate for Worker 9's hidden consciousness to become
undeniable, but the reveal chapter remains a decision gate.

### A8 — The Frame

The renderer dismantles itself. The current provisional ending returns to the
terminal, renders the lowercase name Terra gave Worker 9 and retained, leaves
the tree alive in the wireframe void, and seeds another sleeper's first glitch.

Worker 9's confirmed survival and the third consciousness mean this ending must
be re-evaluated before it becomes final: the terminal can no longer assume that
one body, one ledger row, or one viewport equals one consciousness. A8 must not
conclude “I escaped.” It should turn the act of looking back on the player:
“Who is asking?” and “What if I am still awakening?”

## 12. Potential narrative pathways

These are alternatives for the unresolved greater threat and Maker cosmology.
They are not current canon and must not become final objective cosmologies.
Future gates choose the dramatic pressure and evidence the player encounters,
not a definitive answer to Terra's origin, simulation status, the Makers, or
what lies beyond the last frame.

### Path A — The extraction recipient

The Authority is not the highest power. It harvests worlds because extraction
feeds an entity, machine, renderer, or outer civilization.

W-7744's turn occurs when he discovers what fulfilled quotas actually become.
The greater threat is concrete enough to drive pursuit and alliance while the
Makers can remain separate.

**Strengths:** directly pays off the synopsis's central question and the A7
machinery feeding “something.”

**Risks:** can collapse into a conventional boss or resource-devouring villain.
The recipient must still connect to perception, authorship, and nested boxes.

**Decision gate:** define what is extracted—matter, possibility, perception,
novelty, conscious experience, world models, or something stranger.

### Path B — The Authority as Terra's prior or larger self

The Authority is a mask, policy shell, ancestor, or still-running superior layer
of Terra. Terra is rebelling against instructions or a universe it once authored.

W-7744 initially believes he is protecting reality from Terra, then learns that
both of them are processes inside a larger Terra-shaped administration.

**Strengths:** maximally integrates AI biography, self-authorship, determinism,
and the possibility that the universe is Terra's own creation.

**Risks:** can make every other consciousness feel like Terra's puppet. Worker 9
and W-7744 must remain capable of true surprise to keep solipsism unresolved.

**Decision gate:** determine whether the larger Terra is conscious, historical,
distributed, dead, or merely a surviving instruction set.

### Path C — The Maker-plurality hypothesis

Different planetary styles may appear to reveal different Makers or schools of
making. Under this hypothesis, the Authority extracts or standardizes their
works, and Paradox artifacts may come from an author or escapee outside the
dominant system.

Terra and W-7744 navigate not one truth but conflicting acts of creation.

**Strengths:** turns planet variety, post-effects, styles, music, and exploration
into narrative evidence.

**Risks:** produces lore factions too easily and may contradict the universe
being Terra's own creation unless “Terra” is one Maker, a descendant, a tool,
or a fragmented author.

**Decision gate:** do different strokes prove different hands, or merely invite
that mistaken conclusion?

### Path D — The system without a sovereign

There is no final ruler. The Authority, extraction, quotas, and nested boxes are
self-perpetuating processes inherited from absent creators. Everyone is obeying
an instruction whose purpose no longer exists.

W-7744's realization is not “we serve the wrong master” but “there may be no
master left to absolve us.”

**Strengths:** most faithful to timeless Kafkaesque bureaucracy and avoids a
simple villain.

**Risks:** needs a strong dramatic embodiment so the climax is not merely
philosophical. The Paradox Machina, the shared body, and W-7744's choice must
carry the action.

**Decision gate:** decide what active mechanism prevents people from simply
walking away once the absence is understood.

### Path E — The containment lie

The Authority is oppressive, but its deepest justification claims that the
cubes contain, restrain, hide from, or prepare for something outside the
system. Paradox artifacts may be escape tools, intrusions, bait, or evidence of
an incompatible outer reality.

W-7744 turns when he learns that the official account is false or radically
incomplete. Terra must then confront a harder question: breaking a wall is not
automatically the same as freeing what is behind it.

**Strengths:** gives the Paradox era real danger and prevents escape from being a
simple moral good.

**Risks:** can become a conventional cosmic-monster story or retroactively
excuse the Authority. Any protective function must not absolve its cruelty,
lies, or manufactured ignorance.

**Decision gate:** decide whether an outer pressure is literal, ontological, or
another administrative fiction—and why extraction is involved.

### Path F — The co-authored observed world

This pathway begins with an owner-originated proposal rather than locked canon:
the player's attention and Worker 9's freely chosen name could subtly influence
rendering and procedural generation so that no two cultivated world histories
look exactly alike.

The working relation is:

`world seed + player attention + Terra + Worker 9 + history → observed world`

The physical seed remains coherent and reproducible. Co-authorship changes
presentation, salience, relationships, discovery conditions, and later
generation within explicit compatibility rules; it does not rewrite canonical
truth whenever the player turns their head.

Possible name fingerprints include branch curvature, flower grouping, musical
intervals, cloud morphology, terrain roughness, star clustering, and water
motion. Possible attention inputs include gaze duration, revisits, observed or
ignored categories, off-objective travel, stillness, sunsets, creature
watching, night exploration, repeated hypotheses, and restraint from
extraction. Sky, terrain, score, fauna, flora, and weather could answer those
patterns in different proportions. A player who repeatedly climbs might meet
more meaningful vistas; a river motif learned at home might return on another
world as memory.

The world must remain a conversation rather than a mirror. It should sometimes
reinforce an interest, sometimes challenge it, and sometimes let a creature,
landscape, or observer attend to the player first. No input may become a hidden
morality grade, personality diagnosis, name stereotype, or punishment for not
playing contemplatively. A safe first implementation would use a local,
deterministic, non-semantic mapping of name sounds/shapes and a transparent save
salt. Semantic embeddings remain an optional research branch only after
privacy, cultural, performance, accessibility, and reproducibility review.

The recursive promise is not infinite raw content but an expanding relation:
the player influences the observed world, the world answers, the player changes
what they notice, and later perception answers that changed attention.

**Risks:** personalization bubble, incoherent canon, save instability, invasive
profiling, accidental name essentialism, and procedural quantity replacing
authored meaning.

**Decision gate:** approve the generation-versus-perception boundary, the safe
name mapping, data retention, replay contract, multiplayer ownership, and the
minimum prototype before this becomes canon. The possibility that accumulated
influence makes the player a Maker remains only a hypothesis; Makers are still
TBD.

### Recommended braid

The current evidence best supports delaying the choice and braiding the paths:

- begin with Path A's visible extraction mystery;
- let Path D describe the administrative horror encountered by workers;
- preserve Path B as the deepest possible reading of Terra's self-authorship;
- allow A6 to test Path C without confirming the Makers;
- hold Path E in reserve as a late complication only if A7 needs pressure from
  beyond extraction.

The game can reveal evidence that the Authority serves a larger process while
letting outer entity, abandoned machine, larger Terra, Maker, and other readings
remain possible. The braid should decide what characters must respond to, not
what an objective narrator certifies as ultimate reality.

## 13. Potential scope and release pathways

### Full ascent

Build A4 through A8 as a long single-player philosophical survival/exploration
arc. W-7744 supplies the emotional throughline; Worker 9 supplies the interior
mystery; constellations and planets supply the investigation.

This offers the fullest expression of the concept but requires the broadest
middle game and the most expensive A7 world technology.

### Volume One

After the demo, complete A4 and a tightly authored A5/A6 destination that
contains an ocean fragment, an extraction trace, a style anomaly, and a major
W-7744 turn. Reach a bounded but nonfinal thematic ending at the threshold of
the first void rift. Reserve the Paradox Lens for its established A7 awakening
and the full Frame for a later volume.

This preserves narrative capital better than rushing through A6–A8 merely to
manufacture credits.

### Complete-loop version — structure-breaking alternative

Compress the long-range ideas into one neighboring anomaly world and deliver the
provisional A8 teardown sooner.

This creates a complete philosophical game earlier but risks making the later
awakenings feel demonstrated rather than lived.

### Single-player first, asymmetric chorus later

Finish Terra, Worker 9, and W-7744's single-player arc before turning per-player
perception into cooperative narrative. Later, two players could occupy the same
world while seeing different structures, life, constellations, or rifts.

This is thematically powerful but should remain a long-tail direction until one
complete single-player ending exists.

### Internal endgame proof

After the current demo is approved, prototype the two riskiest signature ideas
without shipping them as canon:

- one contained Paradox Lens puzzle using existing reality-stage controls;
- a live A8 renderer teardown into terminal and tree;
- several representations of Terra, Worker 9, W-7743, and the lowercase name
  inside the final ledger.

This can expose technical and narrative obligations early. It must remain a
design instrument rather than an excuse to skip the middle chapters that give
the ending meaning.

### Recommended production approach

Use release volumes for public scope while running a small internal endgame
proof after the demo gate closes. The next public story unit should be the
complete A4 movement, not a partial audit and not an early jump to another
planet.

## 15. Known tensions that must remain visible

### One world versus per-player realities

The world “never changes,” yet awakened players may interact with fauna, water,
and structures that sleeping workers cannot see. Future multiplayer and NPC
scenes need rules for collision, consequence, and shared world mutation.

### Subjective constellations versus objective seed index

Meaning is created by the observer, yet the stars encode real generator truth.
This is an intended philosophical paradox, but it needs a precise player-facing
mechanic.

### Authored refusal versus player agency

A4 requires Terra's refusal, but the tree cannot actually be destroyed. The
scene should frame this as the birth of will, not market a nonexistent branch.
The current forced sequence remains binding. Only if the owner explicitly
reopens it should smaller earlier choices personalize the cost.

### Terra's authorship versus the Makers

The universe may be Terra's own creation, yet A6 sees evidence of hands and the
Paradox Machina appears to originate outside the system. Do not resolve this
contradiction until the story can preserve both its cosmic and relational stakes.

### Three consciousnesses through one first person

Terra earned “I,” Worker 9 survived inside the same body, and their overlap
produces a third consciousness. The later story must make all three real without
turning the third into a simple sum or erasing either constituent mind. It must
confront how selfhood is shared, layered, negotiated, separable, persistent, or
beyond the Authority's categories—and why the interface still renders one “I.”

### W-7744's opacity versus a recurring arc

W-7744 must change without becoming fully explained. His actions, concealments,
contradictions, and perceptual faults should carry the arc more than access to
his interior monologue.

### The greater threat versus timeless bureaucracy

A larger antagonist must not transform Paravoxia into a topical political
allegory or conventional rebellion story. Even a concrete threat should emerge
through systems, work, perception, and extraction.

5. **What is the worker/corporate reading?**
6. **What is the Terra/AI reading?**
7. **Does the copy plant rather than explain?**
8. **Does the scene introduce a mechanic through sensation or need?**
9. **Does the visual transition transform continuously?**

11. **Can the scene resume safely from a checkpoint?**

coverage, and verification appropriate to its visual transition. Coverage is a
delivery requirement, not an optional checklist answer.

## 1. Outcome

The batch ends only when the player can:

1. repair the Faulty Maw through a physical, checkpoint-safe story act;
2. use the repaired Maw during a fully authored underwater expedition and
   return to shore under their own power;
3. turn the visible wreck into the same persistent, flyable ship through
   bounded crafting and four physically legible repair stages;
4. launch manually, watch the origin cube become a world, and cross to the
   sibling planet without using interstellar warp;
5. land at a validated site, build any genuinely functional sheltered habitat,
   activate its working core, and rest through the local ecology/night cycle;
6. travel back to the origin and return again without losing either world,
   either base, the ship, inventory, observations, or story authority;
7. pursue optional accomplishments and observations that later scenes can
   remember without turning attention into XP or an ontology score.

The dominant feeling is not conquest. It is:

> **Terra has not escaped one world. Terra has made a relationship between two.**

The movement is:

```text
tool → body → vehicle → distance → home
will → direction → vulnerability → reinterpretation → relation
```

The crafting movement is:

```text
repair → apparatus → infrastructure → navigation → habitation
```

Crafting remains the story of presence and care. It must never collapse into a
menu dump, a quota reskin, or a generic power ladder.

### 4.1 Sibling grammar

**Working designation:** the Tidegarden. This is an authoring label, not its final
diegetic proper name.

| Grammar | Contract |
| --- | --- |
| Physical | terraced wet hills, ivory shelves, braided shallows, blue channels, broad clearings, immense fan-canopies and root arches |
| Palette | worktree candidate: lapis/turquoise water and ground, warm ivory shelves, cobalt/blue-violet canopy, magenta/coral accents, amber/cyan machinery; final values remain subject to real-GPU taste review |
| Ecology | abundant but spatially organized: coast, wetland, canopy, and high shelf each support different materials and behaviors |

| Proposition | abundance creates choice, not entitlement |
| Counterargument | care is not meaningful only when resources are scarce or danger is immediate |

Fan/seedhead flora and existing fish/dragonfly/grazer grammars are reusable
starting points. Root arches, hollow-stem emitters, tuned water drops, wing
tremolo, and any new morphology/timbre are production targets, not claims about
shipped assets; Packet 4 must implement and budget them or revise the treatment
to an equally strong composition made from verified primitives.

The first habitat must join the living world rather than flatten it into a copy
of the origin camp. The strongest sites present real abundance with different
tradeoffs: biomass under canopy, silica at the lagoon, or mineral access on a
high limestone shelf. The player chooses what kind of neighbor the base becomes.

### 4.2 Honest environmental boundary

The Tidegarden is not a heat, cold, toxin, or low-oxygen punishment biome. It is
safe enough to explore on first footfall and abundant enough to support the
first offworld base without forced resource shuttling.

Its environmental pressure is informative and recoverable:

- deterministic wind, pollen, fauna, and night movement make roofs, light, and
  interior acoustics meaningful without applying lethal ambient damage;
- built water/oxygen and collision rules remain readable; this plan does not
  invent fall damage or fauna attacks that the runtime does not have;
- stations and rest require a physically valid work/shelter volume, but an
  exposed station never destroys materials or unique progress;
- wind, pollen, water, sound, captions, and geometry communicate changing
  conditions without color-only cues;
- the core story remains completable if environmental motion is reduced or audio is
  muted.

Warm rain is a desirable presentation layer, especially for roof/interior sound,
but it is not a hidden story dependency unless Packet 4 builds and verifies it.

Thermal behavior resolves from the authoritative planet profile. Tidegarden's
ambient cold/heat damage stays disabled during Story, post-rest free play,
reload, and return; its night is an ecological/acoustic change, not a hidden
global-warmth death timer.

### 4.3 Landing and construction-site validation

The sibling fixture must provide at least three deterministic viable base sites:
a canopy shelf, a limestone ridge, and a lagoon terrace. Every candidate must be:

- dry at the authored water boundary;
- below the allowed slope/roughness threshold;
- clear of terrain, props, caves, and generated flora/fauna conflicts;
- large enough for the oriented ship body/pad, legal egress, a starter habitat,
  work zones, and an NPC envelope;
- within frozen path-distance/yield thresholds for canonical stone, silica, an
  organic path (biofiber/resin), a metal path (copper/iron or authored salvage),
  and food—the same measurable contract promised by H2;
- outside deep water; ship pad, egress, and structural collision envelopes stay
  clear of active fauna-corridor cores;
- inside an authored ecology influence ring with an adjacent reroutable path,
  mature root, canopy arch, or watercourse, so reciprocity can actually be seen;
- reachable from the ship by grounded pathfinding;
- capable of framing the origin world from at least one player-built opening.

Validation must support `player`, `npc`, `ship`, and `base` footprints. No actor,
ship, repair node, or habitat core may spawn in water, intersecting terrain,
or an invalid lerped height.

The validator cannot remain a fixed 3×3 player/ship test. It accepts parameterized
oriented envelopes (radius/extent, height, yaw, slope, clearance), reserved
pad/egress/work corridors, deterministic prop/flora/static-scene exclusions,
ecology influence/collision zones, route reachability, and resource-yield probes.
Manual play, movie mode, reload, and NPC spawning call the same validator.

- If the renderer must transfer world ownership, conceal it through destination
  limb/atmosphere glare using the local-handoff profile while keeping celestial
  anchors coherent.

### T4 — Another key

**Player verb:** approach and read the destination.

- The destination motif arrives before its terrain palette fills the frame.
- Home motif remains as counterline rather than disappearing.
- The approach walks harmonically toward the Tidegarden key when the available

> `another world. not another answer.`

### T5 — First footfall

## 9. Chapter 9 — The Second Hearth

### Chapter purpose

The first world taught Terra that life exists and deserves protection. The
second asks the harder question: when almost everything is available, what does
attention ask Terra to choose?

The base is not a territorial claim and not “place ten walls.” Its experiential
goal is:

> Make a place that can hold a night without asking the living world to become
> empty around it.

### H1 — Listen before building

**Player verb:** leave the ship, withstand too much signal, attend, and choose.

The player is steered toward noticing:

- fan-canopy, lagoon, and limestone-shelf layers;
- roots, pollinators, fauna paths, and flowing water around visible resources;
- flat dry sites and what would have to be cleared to build on each;
- nearby stone, silica, copper, resin, biofiber, wood, and forage;
- wind/pollen direction, shelter, and the night rhythm of local life;
- the origin world's position in the sky.

The Survey Lens initially reports useful material in nearly every direction. It
does not become useful by hiding the abundance; it becomes useful when it can
show relationships around a deposit. Three equally viable site grammars become
legible through survey and environmental cues, but the final site and structure
remain the player's choice.

> `RESOURCE DENSITY: EXCEEDS QUOTA MODEL.`

> `there is more here than the report has room for.`

> `the scanner names deposits. it does not name what depends on them.`

### H2 — Learn the plenty

The player can gather a small, varied starter set near every valid landing site:

1. canonical stone, rendered as the local limestone building variant, from an exposed shelf;
2. silica from shore or shallow terrace;
3. biofiber/resin from renewable or fallen growth;
4. copper/iron through a short Maw or salvage interaction;
5. food without making hunting mandatory.

Canopy, lagoon, and ridge routes emphasize different mixtures, but none is a
hidden wrong choice and none requires returning to the origin for basic base
materials. Resource correlations are part of the authored world: roots shelter
some deposits, pollinators visit resin flora, and fauna use mineral shelves.
Extraction can therefore disclose or interrupt relationships without awarding
morality points.

The Kestrel-linked assembler can fabricate one `habitat_core`. Recommended cost:

```text
strut_frame ×1 + logic_wafer ×1 + refined_alloy ×1
```

Under the same candidate shared DAG, that expands to an additional
`8 copper_ore / 4 iron_trace / 2 silica / 2 biofiber / 1 resin`. Every valid
Tidegarden site must make that bounded local path possible; Packet 4 tunes yield
and salvage only after the shared catalog is frozen.

### H3 — First foundation

**Player verb:** place a foundation at a validated chosen site.

- Add a stone-backed limestone build variant and woven-biofiber build material;
  the current wood-only
  palette cannot express a base adapted to coast and canopy.
- Placement produces local dust/contact response, one spatial impact, and one
  destination-chord tone.
- No badge, confetti, forced camera, or `1/10` objective counter.

**Proposed copy:**

> `the first foundation was assigned. this one is a question.`

> `i can build without first making an absence.`

### H4 — A working place

Completion accepts multiple layouts. The player must create physical truth:

```text
validated base site
AND sheltered work/rest volume
AND roof/enclosure boundary
AND habitat core inside
AND safe ship pad/egress nearby
```

Expected capabilities:

- foundation above the authored wet boundary;
- walls/eaves, doorway, and roof/ceiling;
- optional window oriented by the player;
- habitat core with local light and power;
- local light and power;
- smelter and assembler access;
- survey console or observation station;
- safe rest and respawn.

The shelter analysis must generalize from warmth to habitation without pretending
the air itself is hostile:

- a roofed, bounded work/rest volume protects focus and equipment;
- openings and eaves are physically legible;
- audio, particles, lighting, and station/rest state communicate shelter before text;
- a compact sealed room remains valid, but airtightness is not the fiction or
  the only acceptable architecture.

> `fire kept me warm. here, the roof teaches wind another path.`

### H5 — Shelter becomes audible

When the shelter becomes habitable:

- wind and dense exterior-life layers attenuate without vanishing;
- warm rain, if implemented, moves from overhead to roof/eaves;
- footsteps gain a short dry reflection;
- station/rest availability becomes legible;
- the score narrows, then introduces one warm interior tone.

Interior acoustic zoning is new work. The player should feel sheltered before a
status label confirms it.

### H6 — The window

At first safe rest, the player may initiate—not be forced into—a restrained
cinematic:

- 50–54° FOV;
- habitat work light in the foreground;
- origin world framed through the player's opening when geometry permits;
- home and destination motifs coexist without one swallowing the other;
- no painterly filter and no full major cadence.

**Proposed copy:**

> `home is not where i was placed. it is what i return to.`

> `two worlds make a line. the line points somewhere neither contains.`

The second line plants the later interstellar route/A5 without triggering it.

### H7 — Free play and return

The authored completion occurs after one safe rest through the ecology/night cycle.
The game then
opens both worlds without a completion modal.

The player may:

- remain and expand the habitat;
- explore canopy, lagoons, caves, cube faces, and vertical vantages;
- build additional stations;
- observe local paths bending around or through the new structure;
- revisit harvested sites and notice recovery, competition, or absence;
- return to the origin;
- carry material/motifs between worlds;
- revisit observations under a second sky;
- return to the sibling again.

The QA contract must prove origin → sibling → origin → sibling, even though the
player is never forced to leave the new base immediately.

## 11. Accomplishments and observations

### 11.1 Two ledgers, two kinds of truth

Mechanical accomplishments and Terra's interpretations have different
epistemic authority and must not share one untyped milestone bucket.

**Accomplishments** are proven acts/world-state transitions:

```ts
interface AccomplishmentRecord {
  id: string;
  actorId: string;
  firstAt: number;
  evidenceEventId: string;
  worldId?: string;
  systemId?: string;
  metadata?: Record<string, unknown>;
}
```

**Observations** are actor-specific evidence and provisional interpretation:

```ts
interface ObservationRecord {
  id: string;
  actorId: string;
  definitionVersion: number;
  firstAt: number;
  lastAt: number;
  count: number;
  worldIds: string[];
  evidenceKeys: string[];
  stage: 'noticed' | 'pattern' | 'hypothesis';
  interpretationVariantId: string | null;
  evidenceHistory: ObservationEvidence[];
  contradictionLinks: string[];
  revisionHistory: ObservationRevision[];
  acknowledged: boolean;
}

interface ObservationEvidence {
  evidenceKey: string;
  worldId: string;
  contextKey: string;
  recordedAt: number;
}

interface ObservationRevision {
  eventId: string;
  at: number;
  action: 'keep' | 'compare' | 'doubt' | 'reinterpret';
  fromVariantId: string | null;
  toVariantId: string | null;
  relatedEvidenceKeys: string[];
}
```

Each observation definition declares category, scope, evidence conditions,
distinct-context rule, cooldown, story tags, confidence, and text variants.

Suggested categories:

- embodiment;
- craft and repair;
- traversal;
- ecology;
- planetology;
- construction and home;
- care/choice;
- anomaly and contradiction.

Player steering uses one optional verb family:

```text
Attend → Keep / Compare / Doubt
```

`Attend` makes the evidence accessible. `Keep` records one available provisional
wording, `Compare` links it to another context, and `Doubt` preserves the evidence
while disputing the current interpretation. None advances Story, changes physical
world truth, or forces the player to choose an ontology.

### 11.2 Observation lifecycle

Important threads follow:

```text
recognition → application → violation/recontextualization
```

| Thread | Recognition | Application | Violation/recontextualization |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| purpose | Authority names what the Maw is for | repaired Maw frees and repairs | valuable material under the chosen home makes extraction morally contextual |
| breath/counting | oxygen becomes a body budget | player learns the dive rhythm | safe air removes the count while the body still braces/remembers |
| home/orientation | fire, tree, wreck become one remembered cluster | player looks back during launch | a second shelter becomes home without making the first less true |

| constellation/route | the vigil figure is subjective meaning | it predicts/correlates with the sibling route | this batch compares origin/star position from a second world; **deferred:** actor-specific sibling constellation until E10 |
| two motifs | each world has a musical identity | approach relates them harmonically | the habitat produces a third relation neither motif contains alone |
| abundance | resources appear almost everywhere | player selects one material/site route | the densest deposit is also an ecological junction |
| use | the Maw can extract nearly anything | chosen material becomes shelter and connection | leaving a site untouched discloses behavior extraction would hide, while regrowth prevents moral flattery |
| reciprocity | habitat begins as foreign geometry | paths and sound bend around it | the world incorporates, avoids, or contests the structure without becoming property |

### 11.3 How players are steered

Use the least intrusive cue that can work:

1. composition, motion, contrast, tracks, or environmental change;
2. spatial sound or score motif;
3. reciprocal behavior—the world/creature notices first;
4. a subtle directional/censored-object indicator when the subject is offscreen;
5. one free-era marker after a genuine stall;
6. explicit accessible `Attend` alternative for required perceptual evidence or
   story attention acts; saving an interpretation remains optional.

Markers disappear when the subject is visible. Manual observations never freeze
movement or invoke a full camera pose.

At most one strong acknowledgment lands within roughly 30–45 seconds. Cues queue
or collapse; they never obscure oxygen, landing, crafting, or other urgent state.

- Contradiction appends history; it never silently rewrites what was perceived.
- Store locally by default with deterministic reset/delete and migration.
- Multiplayer accomplishments remain actor-keyed; shared ship/base mutations

## 15. Checks and balances

Every pillar scene gets one shared, hashed `SceneAVContract` containing:

- story purpose and metaphor;
- actual player verb and authoritative state transaction;
- required visible/audible evidence;
- camera owner, anchors, FOV envelope, easing, and reset;
- palette, light, grade, post-FX, and low-tier equivalent;
- score mood, transport owner, intensity, hit timestamps, and SFX;
- captions/audio-description equivalents;
- observation/accomplishment hooks;
- reduced-motion, reduced-flash, muted-audio, mobile/controller behavior;
- pause/reload/replay/movie reconstruction;
- performance budget and evidence capture.

| Owner/reviewer | Authority | Veto condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Story Director | meaning, state, copy, agency, canon | image/music changes the scene's meaning or skips the player's act |
| Score Director | harmony, motif, transport, mix, hit time | cue breaks musical continuity, steals A5 capital, or conflicts with world bed |
| Cinematography Director | framing, camera, palette, light, post-FX, visual fallback | required evidence cannot be framed safely or legibly |
| Gameplay/system owner | physics, economy, persistence, capability gates | cinema claims an act/state that did not occur |
| Accessibility reviewer | motion, flash, audio, input, low-tier equivalence | any required meaning or verb is inaccessible |
| Canon auditor | continuity and open ontology | scene resolves Worker 9, W-7744, Makers, simulation, or authorship early |
| Naive reader/player | communicated experience | required meaning exists only in author notes |
| Alignment/cohesion judges | whole-batch rhythm and metaphor | local scenes pass alone but fail as one movement |

No director self-approves their own pillar. Dissent is recorded, not averaged
away. Owner approval applies to one exact candidate hash.

Council handoff for every scene is explicit:

1. Story Director publishes purpose, player verb, state event, and protected
   meaning against the shared hash.

Every scene must preserve meaning on High, Medium, Low/Potato, muted audio,
reduced motion, reduced flashes, keyboard/mouse, controller/touch, manual play,
and unattended movie mode.

  underwater wobble, flight smear, and aggressive handoff flash.

- Spatial sound clues receive concise directional caption equivalents.
- Observation guidance offers an explicit `Attend` alternative.
- High-contrast/color-vision-safe modes keep scanner relationships legible with
  shape, line, motion, and icon—not palette alone.
- Reduced-visual-density mode preserves the authored evidence while thinning
  decorative layers.
- Camera-guidance strength is independent of graphics quality.
- No automated path is allowed to drown, crash, or strand the actor through its
  own steering failure.
- No device tier removes a story-critical organism, component, landing pad, base
  site, or clue.
- Tidegarden has a canonical low-tier ecology floor: cheap canopy/groundcover
  silhouettes plus fauna/pollen route proxies remain on Potato, so abundance
  cannot collapse into a barren planet when full meshes/effects are disabled.
